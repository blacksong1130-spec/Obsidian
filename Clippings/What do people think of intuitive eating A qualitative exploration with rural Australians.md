## Abstract 摘要

Evidence supports that intuitive eating is associated with many indicators of positive physical and mental health, with more recent longitudinal studies establishing causality. Most research, however, comprises either survey data or clinical trials. This study attempts to fill this evidentiary gap by using a qualitative methodology to explore people’s understandings and reactions to intuitive eating, including perceived barriers and enablers to implementation. Three focus group discussions were conducted in a non-metropolitan region of Victoria, Australia, with a total of 23 participants. Focus group transcripts were thematically analysed using an inductive descriptive approach within a constructionist perspective. Findings indicate that the concept of intuitive eating was either unknown or misunderstood. Once intuitive eating was explained, most responses to implementing intuitive eating were negative. Participants felt that having complete choice around what they ate was unlikely to equate to a healthy or balanced diet, at least in the short term. They also argued that because everyday life was not intuitive in its structures, it would be difficult to eat intuitively. Despite these difficulties, participants appreciated that if they were able to overcome the various barriers and achieve a state of intuitive eating, they anticipated a range of long-term benefits to health and weight management. For intuitive eating to become a viable public health approach, this research suggests that intuitive eating needs to be much more widely publicised and better explained, and perhaps renamed. More significantly, people would need assistance with how to eat intuitively given the barriers identified.  
证据支持直觉饮食与许多积极的身心健康指标相关，最近的纵向研究也确立了因果关系。然而，大多数研究主要包括调查数据或临床试验。本研究试图通过定性方法来填补这一证据空白，探讨人们对直觉饮食的理解和反应，包括实施过程中被感知的障碍和助长因素。在澳大利亚维多利亚州一个非大都市区进行了三场焦点小组讨论，共有23名参与者。焦点小组的记录采用归纳描述方法，在建构主义视角下进行了主题分析。研究结果表明，直觉饮食的概念要么是未知的，要么被误解了。一旦直觉饮食被解释清楚，大多数关于实施直觉饮食的反应都是负面的。参与者认为，至少在短期内，完全选择饮食不太可能意味着健康或均衡的饮食。他们还认为，由于日常生活结构不直观，很难凭直觉进食。尽管面临这些困难，参与者们也意识到，如果他们能够克服各种障碍，达到直觉饮食状态，他们预计会对健康和体重管理带来一系列长期益处。为了让直觉饮食成为一种可行的公共卫生方法，这项研究表明，直觉饮食需要更广泛地宣传和更好地解释，甚至可能需要重新命名。更重要的是，鉴于已识别的障碍，人们需要帮助如何直觉饮食。

## Introduction 简介

The evidence is now quite robust that intuitive eating is associated with many indicators of positive physical and mental health \[, \], with more recent longitudinal studies beginning to establish causality \[\]. Intuitive eating is an approach to eating that focuses on responding to innate hunger and satiety signals.  
目前有相当有力的证据表明，直觉饮食与许多积极的身心健康指标相关\[， \]，最近的纵向研究也开始确立因果关系\[\]。直觉饮食是一种注重应对天生饥饿和饱腹感信号的饮食方法。

The concept was originally developed by two nutritional counsellors in 1995 in response to concerns about clients who were distressed by their bodies and inability to lose weight through traditional nutritional manipulations \[\]. Tribole and Resch (1995) developed 10 principles of intuitive eating: (1) reject the diet mentality; (2) honor your hunger; (3) make peace with food; (4) challenge the food police; (5) discover the satisfaction factor; (6) feel your fullness; (7) cope with your emotions with kindness; (8) respect your body; (9) movement–feel the difference; and (10) honor your health–gentle nutrition. In 2006, Tylka developed the Intuitive Eating Scale (IES), which measures individuals’ tendency to follow their physical hunger and satiety cues when determining when, what, and how much to eat. It was based on the work of Tribole and Resch (1995) and consisted of three sub-scales: unconditional permission to eat, eating for physical rather than emotional reasons, and reliance on internal hunger and satiety cues \[\]. In 2013, this scale was refined to include an additional component: body-food congruence \[\].  
这一概念最初由两位营养顾问于 1995 年提出，旨在回应对因自身身体感到困扰且无法通过传统营养调整减重的客户的担忧\[\]。Tribole 和 Resch（1995）提出了直觉饮食的 10 条原则：（1） 拒绝节食心态;（2）尊重你的饥饿;（3）与食物和解;（4）挑战食品警察;（5）发现满意度因子;（6）感受你的充盈;（7）以善意应对情绪;（8）尊重你的身体;（9）运动——感受差异;以及（10）尊重你的健康——温和的营养。2006 年，Tylka 开发了直觉饮食量表（IES），该量表衡量个体在决定何时、吃什么、吃多少时，是否会遵循身体上的饥饿和饱腹感线索。该方法基于 Tribole 和 Resch（1995）的工作，包含三个子量表：无条件允许进食、出于生理而非情感原因进食，以及依赖内在饥饿和饱腹感信号\[\]。2013 年，该量表进一步完善，增加了一个额外因素：身体-食物一致性\[\]。

Most intuitive eating research consists of either survey data testing correlations between intuitive eating and various health indicators, or clinical trials examining the impact of intuitive eating interventions on changes in health measures, mostly amongst women with Body Mass Index (BMI) >25kg/m [^2] \[,, \]. This research indicates that those who practice intuitive eating tend to have better physical and mental health, and individuals with BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> who undergo an intuitive eating intervention have positive outcomes after completing the program. However, we know much less about whether the general population has ever heard of intuitive eating or its principles, what they think of such an approach, or whether they might consider adopting such an approach \[\]. If most people believe that intuitive eating is a bad idea or too difficult to implement, it may have limited value as a public health approach to improving health. This study is an attempt to fill this gap in the evidence by using a qualitative methodology to explore people’s understandings and reactions to intuitive eating, including perceived barriers and enablers to implementation.  
大多数直觉饮食研究包括调查数据，检验直觉饮食与各种健康指标之间的相关性，或临床试验，考察直觉饮食干预对健康指标变化的影响，主要针对身体质量指数（BMI）>25kg/m <sup>2</sup> 的女性\[， ， \]。研究表明，实践直觉饮食的人往往拥有更好的身心健康，且接受直觉饮食干预的 BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> 患者在完成该项目后会获得积极效果。然而，我们对普通民众是否听说过直觉饮食或其原理、他们如何看待这种方法，以及是否会考虑采用这种方法，了解要少得多\[\]。如果大多数人认为直觉饮食是个坏主意或太难实施，那么它作为改善健康的公共卫生方法的价值可能有限。本研究试图通过定性方法来填补证据空白，探讨人们对直觉饮食的理解和反应，包括实施过程中被认为的障碍和助长因素。

## Background 背景

### Intuitive eating and physical and mental health直觉饮食与身心健康

Academic research on intuitive eating is now 25 years old, with the first paper published in 1998 \[\]. Early studies tended to focus on the potential role of intuitive eating in combating the “obesity crisis”. Soon, however, it became clear that, although higher scores on intuitive eating measures were indeed associated with lower BMI, even stronger associations existed between intuitive eating and an array of psychological and psycho-social measures, such as psychological distress, depression, body image, and disordered eating. Indeed, most clinical studies testing an intuitive eating intervention (with, generally, a group of women with BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> found small and sometimes short-lived reductions in weight or BMI, but stronger improvements in participants’ feelings about themselves and their bodies \[, \].  
关于直觉饮食的学术研究已有 25 年历史，首篇论文发表于 1998 年\[\]。早期研究倾向于关注直觉饮食在应对“肥胖危机”中的潜在作用。然而，很快人们发现，虽然直觉饮食得分越高，BMI 越低，但直觉饮食与心理和心理社会指标（如心理困扰、抑郁、身体形象和饮食失调）之间存在更强的关联。事实上，大多数测试直觉饮食干预的临床研究（通常针对 BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> 的女性）发现，体重或 BMI 的减轻幅度虽小且有时短暂，但参与者对自己和身体的感受改善更为显著\[， \]。

As the focus for improving public health has moved away from reducing obesity per se towards improving health and wellbeing, so too has the intuitive eating literature focus shifted. The evidence is now firmly established that eating intuitively is associated with an array of positive physical and mental health indicators \[\]. Moreover, recent longitudinal research found that the causal direction is from intuitive eating to health outcomes. A study of adolescents in the Minneapolis/St Paul area in the U.S. found that greater baseline levels of intuitive eating and increases in intuitive eating from baseline to follow-up were both associated with lower odds of high depressive symptoms, low self-esteem, high body dissatisfaction, unhealthy weight control behaviors (e.g., fasting; skipping meals), extreme weight control behaviors (e.g., taking diet pills; vomiting), and binge eating at eight-year follow-up \[\]. These findings make a clear argument for encouraging the adoption of intuitive eating to improve population health and wellbeing.  
随着改善公共健康的关注点从单纯减少肥胖转向改善健康和福祉，直觉饮食文献的关注点也发生了转变。证据已确凿证明，凭直觉饮食与一系列积极的身心健康指标相关\[\]。此外，最近的纵向研究发现，因果方向是从直觉饮食到健康结果。一项针对美国明尼阿波利斯/圣保罗地区的青少年研究发现，直觉饮食的基线水平越高，且从基线到随访的直觉饮食增加，都与高抑郁症状、低自尊、高身体不满、不健康的体重控制行为（例如禁食;跳餐）、极端体重控制行为（例如， 服用减肥药;呕吐），以及八年随访时的暴饮暴食\[\]。这些发现明确地为鼓励采用直觉饮食以改善人群健康和福祉提供了有力理由。

### Low rates of intuitive eating直觉饮食率低

Rates of intuitive eating, however, appear to be low. Estimates vary, depending on the population and the operationalisation of intuitive eating. The only general population statistics we found–a survey of rural Australians—found the rates of intuitive eating to be 9% for women and 26% for men, using a cut-off of 4.0 or higher (out of 5) on the Tylka Intuitive Eating Scale—a cut-off suggested by Tylka \[\]. A study of 419 women who had given birth within the previous 6–48 months found that 32% were classified as intuitive eaters, with intuitive eating defined as a score above 3.5 on the same Tylka Intuitive Eating Scale \[\]. Much higher rates of intuitive eating were found in a survey of young adults. In this study, however, intuitive eating was operationalised as two items adapted from the Tylka Intuitive Eating Scale: “I trust my body to tell me how much to eat” and “I stop eating when I am full”. Among young adult men, 74.8% indicated that they trusted their body to tell them how much to eat, and 79.1% reported that they stopped eating when full. Among young adult women, 64.8% indicated that they trusted their body to tell them how much to eat, and 76.4% reported that they stopped eating when full \[\]. Men consistently report higher levels of intuitive eating than do women \[\]; this gender difference is particularly large for Caucasians \[\].  
然而，直觉饮食的比例似乎较低。估算会因人群和直觉饮食的实际应用而有所不同。我们找到的唯一一般人口统计数据——一项针对澳大利亚农村地区的调查——显示，直觉饮食的比例女性为 9%，男性为 26%，采用 Tylka 直觉饮食量表的 4.0 分或以上（满分 5 分）的门槛——这是 Tylka 建议的\[\]。一项针对 419 名在 6 至 48 个月内分娩的女性的研究发现，32%被归类为直觉型饮食者，直觉型饮食定义为同一 Tylka 直觉饮食量表得分高于 3.5\[\]。一项针对年轻人的调查发现，直觉饮食的比例要高得多。然而，在这项研究中，直觉饮食被具体化为两项改编自 Tylka 直觉饮食量表的项目：“我相信我的身体会告诉我该吃多少”和“我饱了就停止进食”。在年轻成年男性中，74.8%表示他们信任身体会告诉他们该吃多少，79.1%的人表示他们在饱时停止进食。在年轻成年女性中，64.8%表示她们信任身体会告诉她们该吃多少，76.4%的人表示她们在饱时停止进食\[\]。男性持续报告的直觉饮食水平高于女性\[\];这种性别差异在白人中尤为明显\[\]。

### Feasibility of adopting intuitive eating at a population level在人群层面采用直觉饮食的可行性

Several studies have examined the feasibility of adopting intuitive eating within the confines of clinical trials (e.g., \[–\]). Larkey \[\] investigated dietitians’ perceptions of barriers to the implementation of intuitive eating and Health at Every Size (an intuitive eating and mindfulness program) in community settings. No study we are aware of, however, has investigated the feasibility of adopting intuitive eating in the general population. In addition, little research exists regarding the extent to which the general public is aware of or understands intuitive eating. This is despite recent public interest in and exposure to body positivity, including the naming of body image activist Taryn Brumfitt as the 2023 Australian of the Year. Body positivity and intuitive eating share common elements, such as respecting one’s body \[\].  
多项研究探讨了在临床试验范围内采用直觉饮食的可行性（例如，\[–\]）。Larkey \[\] 调查了营养师对社区环境中实施直觉饮食和“健康饮食”（一种直觉饮食与正念项目）的障碍的看法。然而，我们所知的没有研究探讨在普通人群中采用直觉饮食的可行性。此外，关于公众对直觉饮食的认知程度的研究也很少。尽管公众最近对身体积极的关注和曝光，包括身体形象活动家塔琳·布鲁姆菲特被评为 2023 年澳大利亚年度人物，这一成就依然存在。身体积极与直觉饮食有共同点，比如尊重自己的身体\[\]。

The aim of this study was to explore peoples’ understanding of and reactions to intuitive eating, including perceived barriers and enablers to implementation, to provide a foundational understanding upon which public health policy around healthful eating can be built.  
本研究旨在探讨人们对直觉饮食的理解和反应，包括实施过程中被认为的障碍和助力因素，以提供建立健康饮食公共卫生政策的基础理解。

## Methods 方法

### Study design and participants研究设计与参与者

Three focus group discussions were conducted with a total of 23 participants. Demographic characteristics of each group consisted of: (1) young women, aged 18–24, with no children; (2) women aged 35–45 with primary school aged children; and (3) men, aged 35–50, living with a partner and with pre- or primary school aged children. These three group demographics were selected to target significant age and life-stages in which shifts in eating behaviours may occur \[\]. The groups were conducted in a hotel conference facility in Bendigo, a regional centre of Victoria, Australia, with participants recruited from Bendigo city and outlying areas. A non-metropolitan area was chosen for this research because rates of people with a BMI > 30kg/m <sup>2</sup> are generally higher in non-metropolitan areas in Australia \[\].  
共进行了三场焦点小组讨论，共有 23 名参与者。各组的人口特征包括：（1）18 至 24 岁、无子女的年轻女性;（2）35 至 45 岁且有小学年龄子女的女性;以及（3）35 至 50 岁的男性，与伴侣同住，且有学龄前或小学年龄的孩子。这三组人口统计学被选中针对可能发生饮食行为变化的重要年龄和生命阶段\[\]。小组在澳大利亚维多利亚州本迪戈的一家酒店会议设施内举行，参与者来自本迪戈市及周边地区。选择非都市区进行研究是因为澳大利亚 BMI > 30kg/m <sup>2</sup> 人群的发病率通常在非都市区更高\[\]。

The focus group discussions were guided by a Discussion Guide jointly developed by MM, NV, and ED. The Discussion Guide was not pilot tested. Repeat interviews were not carried out. The number of focus groups and participants was based on inclusion of an adequate representation of key demographic groups and budget, rather than data saturation. Therefore, findings should be considered preliminary.  
焦点小组讨论由 MM、NV 和 ED 联合开发的讨论指南指导。讨论指南未进行试点测试。未进行重复访谈。焦点小组和参与者的数量基于关键人口群体和预算的充分代表性，而非数据饱和。因此，调查结果应被视为初步。

### Focus group moderator and reflexivity焦点小组主持人与反思性

MM conducted the three focus groups. MM holds a Graduate Diploma in Applied Psychology and a Diploma of Applied Science (Naturopathy). He has 20 years’ experience conducting qualitative research. At the time of the study, MM was Director of Qualitative Research at the Social Research Centre. MM is male and uses he/him pronouns. Prior to study commencement, MM had no relationship with the focus group participants. Participants knew nothing about the researcher, other than that he would be talking to them about their eating habits and how they made decisions around food. At the time of conducting the focus groups, MM’s BMI was approximately 25–26 kg/m <sup>2</sup>. He was using an intuitive eating approach some of the time.  
MM 主持了三场焦点小组。MM 拥有应用心理学研究生文凭和应用科学（自然疗法）文凭。他拥有 20 年进行定性研究的经验。研究进行时，MM 担任社会研究中心定性研究主任。MM 是男性，使用 he/him 代词。在研究开始之前，MM 与焦点小组参与者没有任何关系。参与者对研究人员一无所知，只知道他会与他们谈论他们的饮食习惯以及他们如何做出关于食物的决策。在进行焦点小组时，MM 的 BMI 约为 25–26 公斤/米 <sup>2</sup> 。他有时会用直觉式进食。

### Procedure 程序

Recruitment was conducted by a professional recruitment agency over the telephone from databases of people who had previously agreed to take part in market and social research. During the recruitment process, a screening questionnaire was used that included several questions about body weight and history of weight loss diets. To ensure a good mix of respondents for the discussions, the following quotas were set for each group: at least two in each group who had previously been on a weight loss diet and at least two who had never been on a weight loss diet; and at least three in each group who reported that they were “over my most healthy weight”. Each participant provided oral informed consent before participation. Prior to the start of each focus group, MM provided a verbal explanation of the research and its purpose, and asked participants if they consented to having the discussion recorded and for the recordings to be used for the purposes of analysis and producing papers and reports. Participants were paid AUS$70. This approach was approved by the IRB.

The concept of intuitive eating was introduced to each of the groups following a discussion about diets and eating patterns. ### Data collection 数据收集

The focus groups and coding were conducted in 2010. Only MM and the participants were present during the discussions. With the permission of participants, all research sessions were recorded and transcribed. The topic of intuitive eating was introduced first by name, followed by a definition. Participants were then asked what they thought about such an approach, and any barriers they saw in implementing such an approach. Each focus group lasted approximately 90 minutes. After each focus group, MM documented his initial reflections. Transcripts were not returned to participants for comment and/or correction.  
焦点小组和编码于 2010 年进行。讨论中只有 MM 和参与者出席。经参与者同意，所有研究环节均被录音并转录。直觉饮食这个话题最初被介绍为名称，随后才有定义。随后，参与者被问及对这种方法的看法，以及他们在实施过程中发现的任何障碍。每个焦点小组大约持续 90 分钟。每次焦点小组结束后，MM 都会记录他最初的反思。未将文字记录退回给参与者以供评论和/或更正。

### Data analysis 数据分析

The transcriptions were thematically analysed by MM using an inductive descriptive approach \[\] located within a constructionist perspective \[\], following the general step-by-step approach outlined in Murphy et al \[\]. An inductive descriptive approach was used to allow the data to drive the analysis, which was appropriate given the exploratory and descriptive purpose of the study. Themes were created by grouping codes with a central organising concept \[, \]. A constructionist perspective assumes that multiple views of reality exist and attempts to understand the social construction of reality from the viewpoint of the study participants \[\].  
MM 采用归纳描述方法\[\]对转录进行主题分析，采用建构主义视角\[\]，遵循 Murphy 等人\[\]所述的一般逐步方法。采用归纳描述方法，使数据驱动分析，这符合研究探索性和描述性目的。主题通过将代码与一个中心组织概念分组来创建\[， \]。建构主义观点假设存在多种现实观，并试图从研究参与者的视角理解社会对现实的建构\[\]。

The analysis approach involved the following steps: (1) reading a sample of transcriptions to identify initial key themes and subthemes; (2) preparing a coding guide based on these themes and sub-themes; (3) reading/re-reading each transcript and coding against the guide, adjusting themes and sub-themes as necessary; (4) collating coded responses for each theme and subtheme. The results were discussed with the other co-authors and the first author also read the transcripts. All three authors agreed with the findings.  
分析方法包括以下步骤：（1）阅读转录样本以确定初始的关键主题和子主题;（2）基于这些主题和子主题准备编码指南;（3）阅读/重读每份文字稿，并根据指南进行编码，根据需要调整主题和子主题;（4）整理每个主题和子主题的编码回答。结果与其他合著者讨论，第一作者也阅读了记录。三位作者都同意这一发现。

A selection of quotes has been included to illustrate themes; they do not necessarily reflect the specific attitudes of or differences between each demographic group. Each quote has been identified according to the group in which it was said. Participants did not provide feedback on the findings.  
书中包含了一些引言以说明主题;它们不一定反映每个人口群体的具体态度或差异。每句话都根据说出时的群体进行识别。参与者未对研究结果提供反馈。

### Initial perceptions (prior to explanation)初步感知（解释前）

Prior to participation in the focus groups, none of the participants had ever heard of intuitive eating. Initial reactions to the term were indicative of responses that might be expected if these participants were to come across such a reference in the public domain. In response to the term, the common perception was that intuitive eating was about eating what you feel like. Overall, participants believed that this was unlikely to be a good thing, as they believed that they were more likely to desire food that was not healthy (i.e., high in fat and sugar and based on looks and taste rather than nutrition) than food that was consistent with a healthy diet. Similarly, some were concerned that intuitive eating would be the same as responding to cravings, and based on their experience, cravings tended to be for foods that were not healthy.  
在参与焦点小组之前，参与者中没有人听说过直觉饮食。对该词的初步反应表明，如果这些参与者在公共领域遇到此类引用，可能会有相应的反应。针对这个词，普遍的看法是直觉饮食就是吃你想吃的东西。总体而言，参与者认为这不太可能是好事，因为他们更倾向于渴望不健康的食物（即高脂肪和高糖分，基于外观和口感而非营养），而非符合健康饮食的食物。同样，有些人担心直觉饮食会等同于对渴望的反应，根据他们的经验，渴望往往是对不健康食物的反应。

### Perceived barriers to eating intuitively直觉饮食的感知障碍

While some of the immediate responses presented above were barriers to the idea of intuitive eating, the notion of barriers was specifically probed and explored in the groups. Perceived barriers included food availability, overcoming existing food habits, difficulty differentiating between desire and cravings, removing temptations, and life structures and patterns that limit flexibility.  
虽然上述一些直接反应成为直觉饮食的障碍，但障碍的概念在小组中被特别探讨和探讨。被感知的障碍包括食物供应、克服现有的饮食习惯、难以区分欲望与渴望、消除诱惑，以及限制灵活性的生活结构和模式。

#### Food availability. 食物供应。

Some participants felt that a major barrier to adopting intuitive eating was that they did not have a sufficient range of foods available to make intuitive choices. They felt that they would only be able to follow their intuition if all foods were available all of the time. This was thought to be a particular problem for certain groups of people; for example, for people who lived in more rural or remote areas.  
一些参与者认为，采用直觉饮食的主要障碍是他们没有足够的食物选择来做出直觉选择。他们认为只有当所有食物随时都能供应时，他们才能遵循直觉。这被认为是某些群体特别的问题;例如，对于生活在更偏远或偏远地区的人来说。

> *“I don’t think the right foods are available to a lot of people.”  
> “我觉得合适的食物对很多人来说并不常见。”*
> 
> *(Women*, *35–45)*  
> *（女性* ， *35–45）*

#### Overcoming existing food habits.克服现有的饮食习惯。

A major issue for participants across all groups was the idea that they were already accustomed to certain foods and eating practices, and that any habits or pre-existing conditioning would interrupt the capacity for intuition. Some believed that they were already conditioned to eat certain things, and that it would take a long time to overcome their existing non-intuitive diets and approaches to eating.  
所有参与者面临的一个主要问题是，他们已经习惯了某些食物和饮食习惯，任何习惯或先天条件反射都会干扰直觉能力。有些人认为自己已经习惯了吃某些东西，克服现有的非直觉饮食和饮食方式需要很长时间。

> *“It might not help you nutrition wise if you are* … *too busy eating your cheese burger or whatever*, *you might not really have the sense of mind to actually want the foods that are going to help you feel a bit more motivated or a bit healthier*, *or what not*.*”*  
> *“如果你是* ......你可能忙着 *吃芝士汉堡什么* 的，可能 *根本没有心思去尝试那些能让你更有动力、更健康* *的食物。* *“*
> 
> *(Women*, *18–24)*  
> *（女子* ， *18–24 岁）*
> 
> *“Well*, *my body likes what I like*.*”*  
> *“嗯* ， *我的身体喜欢我喜欢的* 。 *“*
> 
> *(Women*, *18–24) \[in response to comment that intuitive eating sounds like just eating whatever you like\]*  
> *（女性* ， *18–24 岁）\[回应“直觉饮食听起来就是随心所欲吃”的评论\]*

Related to this issue, some participants talked about having quite unhealthy approaches to food, including craving certain foods and having unhealthy eating patterns, and believed that unless these were addressed first, they would not be able to make intuitive choices.  
与此相关的问题，一些参与者谈到了对食物的不健康态度，包括渴望某些食物和不健康的饮食模式，他们认为除非先解决这些问题，否则他们无法做出直觉选择。

#### Difficulty differentiating between desire and cravings.难以区分欲望和渴望。

It was apparent that a substantial limitation of the concept of intuitive eating was that participants were not sure how to distinguish between intuition, desire, and cravings. Some participants believed that eating what one wants and what one needs were mostly the same thing, but others were not as confident, and some felt they were very separate concepts. Across the groups, there was a strong and consistent tendency to believe that if they were eating what their body told them to, as per the intuitive eating definition, then they would eat cake and chips all the time. Some also felt that they would first need to re-educate themselves to not overeat.  
很明显，直觉饮食概念的一个重大局限是参与者不确定如何区分直觉、欲望和渴望。一些参与者认为吃自己想吃的东西和需要的东西大多是一样的，但也有人不那么自信，还有些人觉得两者是完全不同的概念。在各个群体中，普遍且一致地认为，如果他们按照身体的直觉饮食定义去吃，那么他们就会一直吃蛋糕和薯片。还有人觉得，首先需要重新教育自己，避免暴饮暴食。

> *“You’d have to change your thinking*. *Like*, *if you’ve got a plate of food in front of you*, *you feel that you’ve got to eat the whole lot*, *not waste any*.*”*  
> *“你得改变想法* 。 *比如* ， *如果你面前有一盘食物* ， *你会觉得必须把全部吃* 完， *不能浪费任何一块* 。 *“*

一些年轻女性担心她们或她们的朋友会利用这些原则为饥饿节食辩护。他们评论说，有些人认识的人会用类似描述直觉饮食的词语来为不吃饭找借口。从这个角度看，他们担心直觉饮食的理念可能会被滥用，从而让人们为不健康的饮食行为找借口。

Several participants discussed how this was relevant in terms of both the foods that their children ate and the times of their eating. In essence, they believed that it was their responsibility as parents to ensure that their children ate well, rather than to allow them to eat what they wanted.  
一些家长担心这个概念如何应用于他们的孩子。他们表示，如果自己难以理解这个概念不仅仅是随心所欲地吃，他们就无法理解这如何适用于自己的孩子。几位参与者讨论了这与孩子所吃食物和用餐时间的相关性。本质上，他们认为作为父母，他们有责任确保孩子吃得好，而不是让他们随心所欲地吃东西。

#### Removing temptations. 消除诱惑。

Many participants felt that, in order to be able to make intuitive choices, they would need to develop ways of resisting the temptations that they currently experienced. These included overcoming the impact of advertising and marketing of foods, and ensuring that they did not have easy access to the foods they believed they had cravings for (e.g., chocolate, sweets, etc.). This would require different approaches to shopping and the stocking of their refrigerators and pantries. Finally, they discussed the need to overcome what they regarded as normal reactions to foods in their immediate environment, including physiological responses to the sight and smell of other peoples’ foods. One component of this issue would be working out how to deal with social events, such as eating out.  
许多参与者认为，为了能够做出直觉选择，他们需要发展出抵抗当前诱惑的方法。这些包括克服食品广告和营销的影响，确保他们无法轻易获得他们认为渴望的食物（如巧克力、甜食等）。这需要不同的购物方式以及冰箱和储藏室的储备。最后，他们讨论了克服他们认为对周围食物正常反应的必要性，包括对他人食物视觉和气味的生理反应。这个问题的一个组成部分是如何应对社交活动，比如外出就餐。

#### Life structures and patterns limit flexibility.生活结构和模式限制了灵活性。

As indicated by people’s initial reactions to the definition of intuitive eating, there was a strong and consistent belief that the practice of intuitive eating was not compatible with normal daily routines and structures, and would be difficult to maintain. This was thought to be especially problematic in terms of school and work, which often included set starting, finishing, and meal break times. Other activities, such as sports and social activities, were also commonly governed by set times. Participants explained that their food choices and timing needed to be organised around these, thus making a truly intuitive approach to eating impossible.  
正如人们对直觉饮食定义的初步反应所显示，普遍且一致地认为直觉饮食的实践与正常的日常作息和结构不兼容，且难以维持。这在学校和工作方面尤其存在问题，因为工作常常涉及课程的开始、结束和用餐时间。其他活动，如体育和社交活动，也通常有固定时间。参与者解释说，他们的饮食选择和时间安排必须围绕这些因素进行，因此实现真正直观的进食方式变得不可能。

> *(Women*, *18–24)*

### Perceived benefits and motivators to eating intuitively直觉饮食的感知益处和动机

Despite these perceived difficulties, most participants felt that if they were able to overcome the barriers and achieve a state of intuitive eating, there would be a range of benefits. Overall, the idea of living more in tune with one’s body was considered appealing.  
尽管存在这些困难，大多数参与者认为如果能够克服障碍，达到直觉饮食状态，将会带来多种益处。总体来说，更贴近身体的生活方式被认为很有吸引力。

> *“Yeah*, *I think it would be good*. *I think that it would be…you would feel like you were… in tune*.*”*  
> *“是的* ， *我觉得挺好的* 。 *我觉得会是......你会觉得自己是......心灵同步* 。 *“*
> 
> *(Women*, *35–45)*  
> *（女性* ， *35–45）*

Participants felt that, with an intuitive eating approach, they would eventually improve their food habits, with the main benefits that they would end up eating smaller portions and a healthier range of foods. As a consequence, they believed that they would eventually attain a better diet and healthier body weight. There was some understanding that it would take time for eating to become intuitive. These thoughts were premised on the idea documented previously, that people’s existing food habits had developed patterns that were not intuitive, and that it would take some time to overcome these. Some appreciated that over time they would eat different foods and adopt different eating patterns, and that these approaches would eventually become their new pattern of eating.

## Discussion 讨论

The research evidence is increasingly clear that intuitive eating may be helpful in supporting mental and physical health in the general population. Cross sectional surveys indicate that people who eat intuitively also have lower BMIs, and score higher on an array of physical and mental health indicators \[,, \]. People with BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> who complete intuitive eating programs tend to reduce or maintain their BMI, and improve on indicators of mental and physical health \[,, \]. However, these findings are of little use from a public health perspective if the general population is unaware of intuitive eating, uninterested in becoming intuitive eaters, or deems such an approach too difficult or impractical to implement. It is therefore crucial to understand people’s reactions to the concept of intuitive eating; yet, little research has been conducted on this topic. Erhardt \[\], for example, states: “There is a distinct lack of qualitative research exploring people’s experiences of learning to eat more intuitively”.  
研究证据越来越明确，直觉饮食可能有助于支持普通人群的心理和身体健康。横断面调查显示，凭直觉饮食的人 BMI 较低，且在多种身体和心理健康指标上得分更高\[， ， \]。BMI >25kg/m <sup>2</sup> 的人完成直觉饮食计划后，往往能降低或维持 BMI，并在心理和身体健康指标上有所改善\[， ， \]。然而，如果普通民众对直觉饮食一无所知、不愿成为直觉饮食者，或认为这种方法过于困难或不切实际，这些发现从公共卫生角度看几乎没有用处。因此，理解人们对直觉饮食概念的反应至关重要;然而，关于这一主题的研究却很少。例如，Erhardt \[\]指出：“目前明显缺乏探讨人们更直观学习饮食体验的定性研究。”

Findings from this study suggest significant barriers to public adoption of an intuitive eating approach. The concept itself was unknown and generally misunderstood by focus group participants. None of the participants had ever previously heard of this approach to eating. Most thought it probably referred to eating whatever one felt like, which most associated with eating in response to cravings and/or eating unhealthy, “junk” foods. These findings suggest that significant public education would be necessary to clarity the concept of intuitive eating.  
本研究结果表明，公众采用直观饮食方法存在重大障碍。该概念本身未知，且通常被焦点小组参与者误解。参与者中没有人以前听说过这种饮食方式。大多数人认为这可能指的是随心所欲地吃东西，而大多数人认为这与满足渴望或吃不健康的“垃圾”食品有关。这些发现表明，有必要进行大量公众教育，以澄清直觉饮食的概念。

Somewhat heartening is research indicating that registered dietitians (in the United States) are increasingly knowledgeable about and favourably perceive intuitive eating \[\]. This would suggest that people who seek dietary assistance may be informed about intuitive eating. However, a more recent experimental study of 31 registered dietitians found that, although most had heard of intuitive eating and believed they were incorporating some elements of intuitive eating into their practice, when presented with two case studies with identical medical indications, they recommended patients with higher BMIs lose weight in a manner that contradicted the main principles of intuitive eating \[\].  
令人稍感欣慰的是，研究显示美国注册营养师对直觉饮食的了解和认可程度日益提升\[\]。这表明寻求饮食帮助的人可能已经了解了直觉饮食。然而，一项针对 31 名注册营养师的最新实验研究发现，尽管大多数人听说过直觉饮食，并认为他们在实践中融入了某些元素，但当面对两个具有相同医学适应症的案例时，他们建议 BMI 较高的患者减重方式与直觉饮食的主要原则相矛盾\[\]。

Once intuitive eating was explained, most responses to implementing such an approach were negative. Participants felt that having complete choice around what they ate was unlikely to equate to a healthy or balanced diet, at least in the short term. They also argued that because everyday life was not intuitive in its structures, it would be difficult to eat intuitively. Switching to intuitive eating, therefore, was thought to require an entire lifestyle change, rather than just a change in food choices and eating patterns. They also argued that existing food patterns and preferences would need to be addressed before an intuitive approach to eating could be adopted. This would require addressing cravings and factors that affected taste preferences. In addition, participants discussed the fact that external factors outside the control of the individual influenced food choices, including advertising and marketing of food products and the availability of foods. Moreover, various social structures required set times (e.g., work, school, sports, and other social activities) and that food choices and eating times needed to be coordinated around these, which participants believed would make intuitive eating impractical if not impossible. Finally, parents were concerned about how the concept could be applied to children, as some believed that children required set time structures for meals and did not have the knowledge or awareness to make appropriate food choices.  
一旦解释了直觉饮食，大多数对实施这种方法的反应都是负面的。参与者认为，至少在短期内，完全选择饮食不太可能意味着健康或均衡的饮食。他们还认为，由于日常生活结构不直观，很难凭直觉进食。因此，转向直觉饮食被认为需要彻底改变生活方式，而不仅仅是改变饮食选择和饮食习惯。他们还认为，在采用直观的饮食方法之前，必须先解决现有的食物模式和偏好。这需要解决对味觉偏好的渴望和影响口味偏好的因素。此外，参与者讨论了外部因素影响了个人无法控制的食物选择，包括食品的广告和营销以及食品的可获得性。此外，各种社会结构要求固定时间（如工作、学校、体育及其他社交活动），且食物选择和用餐时间必须围绕这些时间协调，参与者认为这会使直觉饮食变得不切实际甚至不可能。最后，家长们担心这一概念如何应用于儿童，因为有人认为孩子需要固定的用餐时间结构，且缺乏做出适当食物选择的知识或意识。

Some of these same concerns are reflected in the literature from small intuitive eating trials. A qualitative study with 11 middle-aged women learning to eat intuitively, for example, found that the women were able to learn to eat more intuitively. However, they encountered social and environmental barriers, with the ‘unconditional permission to eat’ aspect of intuitive eating the most challenging \[\]. A U.S. study with non-Hispanic black men with Type 2 diabetes identified multiple barriers to healthy eating (not specifically intuitive eating), including hard-to-break habits, limited resources and availability of food at home and in neighbourhood grocery stores, and perceived poor communication with health care professionals \[\].  
这些担忧在小型直觉饮食试验的文献中也有体现。例如，一项针对 11 名中年女性的定性研究发现，她们能够更直观地学会饮食。然而，他们遇到了社会和环境障碍，其中“无条件允许进食”这一直观饮食的部分最具挑战性\[\]。一项针对非西班牙裔黑人 2 型糖尿病男性的研究发现，健康饮食（非直觉饮食）存在多重障碍，包括难以改掉的习惯、家庭和邻里杂货店食物资源有限及供应不足，以及与医疗专业人员沟通不良的感知\[\]。

Despite these difficulties, participants appreciated that if they were able to overcome the various barriers and achieve a state of intuitive eating, then they anticipated a range of long-term benefits in regard to health and weight management. The fact that many participants expressed a desire to be provided with evidence that intuitive eating “works” suggests that, if provided with this evidence, they might be convinced to try to change their eating behaviours. However, participants also wanted evidence regarding how eating intuitively might affect people over time in terms of health and weight management. Most of the existing research, however, consists of either a single point in time measure, or follows people for only a short period of time–usually for between six months and two years. This last finding supports previous recommendations regarding the need to conduct research with longer-term outcomes \[\].  
尽管面临这些困难，参与者仍然意识到，如果他们能够克服各种障碍，达到直觉饮食状态，那么他们预计在健康和体重管理方面会获得一系列长期益处。许多参与者表示希望获得直觉饮食“有效”的证据，这表明如果提供这些证据，他们可能会被说服尝试改变饮食行为。然而，参与者也希望获得关于直觉饮食如何随着时间推移影响人们的健康和体重管理的证据。然而，现有大多数研究要么只用一个时间点来衡量，要么只跟踪人群很短的时间——通常在六个月到两年之间。这一最后发现支持了此前关于需要开展具有长期结果的研究的建议\[\]。

If intuitive eating were to become a viable public health approach, this research suggests that people would need assistance in overcoming their existing habits and food preferences, and developing better insight into their bodies’ needs, desires, and cravings. The structural barriers, however, would likely continue to be substantial. People would need to learn how to adapt the concept of intuitive eating to fit in with their work, family, and social schedules. As with many behavioural changes, eating intuitively would likely require a gradual shift in behaviours and attitudes. A starting point would be implementation of a public health campaign educating people about intuitive eating and its connections with better health. Another step would be specific training of dietitians, GPs, and other medical and allied health professionals in intuitive eating and its potential benefits to their clients. Access to such services must also be examined, unless we want to further exacerbate the health divide been the rich and poor.  
如果直觉饮食成为一种可行的公共卫生方法，这项研究表明，人们需要帮助克服现有的习惯和饮食偏好，并更好地洞察自身身体的需求、欲望和渴望。然而，结构性障碍可能依然很大。人们需要学会如何调整直觉饮食的概念，以适应自己的工作、家庭和社交时间表。像许多行为改变一样，直觉饮食可能需要行为和态度的逐步转变。一个起点是开展一项公共卫生宣传活动，教育人们了解直觉饮食及其与健康关系。另一个步骤是对营养师、全科医生及其他医疗和相关健康专业人员进行直觉饮食及其对客户潜在益处的专项培训。必须审视这些服务的可及性，否则我们会进一步加剧贫富之间的健康差距。

Most significantly, however, are the changes that must be made to the food environment if intuitive eating is ever to become a real option for most people. Intuitive eating requires choice of food, yet ample evidence demonstrates that true choice is often not available, and that more healthful and less healthful foods do not exist on an even playing field \[\]. Supermarkets and other food retail outlets, for example, preference highly processed foods over fresh foods in their placement and pricing \[–\]. Many people, and particularly those living in lower socio-economic areas, live in “food deserts” and/or “food swamps”—areas with no access to a nearby supermarket or with a high ratio of fast-food outlets to fresh food outlets \[, \]. Advertising spending favours highly processed foods over fresh foods by a large margin \[\]. And the cost of fresh food has risen more rapidly than the cost of highly processed food \[–\].  
然而，最重要的是，如果直觉饮食想让大多数人真正成为一种选择，必须对食物环境做出改变。直觉饮食需要选择食物，但大量证据表明，真正的选择往往不存在，且更健康和更不健康的食物并不存在于公平竞争的起跑线\[\]。例如，超市和其他食品零售店在摆放和定价上更倾向于高度加工食品而非新鲜食品\[–\]。许多人，尤其是生活在低社会经济阶层的人，生活在“食物沙漠”和/或“食物沼泽”——这些地区附近没有超市，或者快餐店与新鲜食品店的比例很高\[， \]。广告支出大幅偏向高度加工食品而非新鲜食品\[\]。而且新鲜食品的成本上涨速度快于高度加工食品的成本\[–\]。

### Study strengths and limitations学习优势与局限性

This study is the first we are aware of to explore the concept of intuitive eating with a general population. Its focus on people living in a regional town is an important feature given that research is often conducted in major cities, which can have a very different food environment and lifestyle. This study was conducted with 23 people from three demographic groups from a small area in regional Victoria, Australia. Additional research with larger numbers and other types of people, both within and outside of Australia, would be useful to understand the extent to which these findings hold more broadly. Identified barriers and enablers to uptake of intuitive eating are likely to be similar in other western populations.  
这项研究是我们已知的首次在普通人群中探讨直觉饮食概念的项目。它聚焦于居住在区域城镇的人群是一个重要特点，因为研究通常在大城市进行，而这些城市的饮食环境和生活方式可能截然不同。这项研究对来自澳大利亚维多利亚州一个小区域的三个人口群体的23人进行了。对更多人及其他类型人群（包括澳大利亚内外）进行更多研究，有助于理解这些发现在更广泛的范围内的适用性。其他西方人群中，发现的接受直觉饮食的障碍和助长因素很可能类似。

The definition of intuitive eating provided to participants included the statement: “The basic idea \[of intuitive eating\] is that, if listened to, the body intuitively ‘knows’ how much and what kinds of food to eat, both to maintain a healthy diet and an appropriate weight.” Participants may have interpreted ‘an appropriate weight’ as a lower body weight according to societal norms. A single researcher conducted the focus groups and analysed the data. With thematic analysis, coding quality is not dependent on multiple coders \[\]. The results were discussed with the other co-authors and the first author also read the transcripts. All three authors agreed with the findings.  
向参与者提供的直觉饮食定义中包括：“直觉饮食的基本思想是，如果倾听，身体会直觉地'知道'该吃多少、吃什么样的食物，既能维持健康饮食，也能保持合适体重。”参与者可能将“适当体重”理解为根据社会规范的较低体重。一位研究人员负责开展焦点小组并分析数据。通过主题分析，编码质量不依赖于多个编码者\[\]。结果与其他合著者讨论，第一作者也阅读了记录。三位作者都同意这一发现。

Finally, the focus groups were conducted in 2010 and we cannot be confident that the findings would be identical now. In particular, these findings do not reflect the potential impact on attitudes towards intuitive eating and eating choices of the COVID pandemic and associated lockdowns.  
最后，焦点小组是在2010年进行的，我们无法保证现在的结果会完全相同。特别是，这些发现并未反映新冠疫情及相关封锁对直觉饮食和饮食选择态度的潜在影响。

## Conclusion 结论

Intuitive eating has great potential for improving people’s physical and mental health. However, any public health approach must consider the realities of people’s lives, including both personal and structural barriers.  
直觉饮食在改善人们的身心健康方面有巨大潜力。然而，任何公共卫生方法都必须考虑人们生活的现实，包括个人和结构性障碍。
