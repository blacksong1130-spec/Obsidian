## 

Hello, my name is Wei Jie Li, and this project is called **FoodWitness**.  
大家好，我叫 Wei Jie Li，这个项目名为 **FoodWitness《食物见证》**。

FoodWitness begins with a simple statement: **The shelf is not neutral.**  
FoodWitness 从一个简单的判断出发：**货架从来不是中立的。**

When people develop diet-related chronic illnesses, unhealthy eating is often described as a personal failure.  
当人们患上与饮食有关的慢性疾病时，不健康饮食往往被解释为个人的失败。

We say that people lack knowledge, discipline or willpower.  
我们会认为，这是因为人们缺乏知识、自律或意志力。

But this explanation ignores the environment in which food choices are made.  
但这种解释忽略了食品选择发生的环境。

Before a shopper makes a decision, price, promotion, packaging, product placement and convenience have already shaped the available choices.  
在消费者作出决定之前，价格、促销、包装、货架位置和便利程度，已经影响了他们的选择。

Meanwhile, information about processing, health consequences, producer income and environmental impact often remains fragmented or invisible.  
与此同时，与加工过程、健康后果、生产者收入和环境影响有关的信息，通常是零散的，甚至完全不可见。

So FoodWitness changes the question.  
因此，FoodWitness 重新提出了问题。

Instead of asking, “Why can’t people control themselves?”  
它不再问：“为什么人们不能控制自己？”

It asks, “Why is the food environment designed this way?”  
它转而问：“为什么我们的食品环境会被设计成这样？”

Today, chronic illness is becoming a larger part of everyday life.  
如今，慢性疾病正在越来越深地进入我们的日常生活。

Many people feel confused about what is healthy, what information they can trust and whether healthier choices are affordable.  
许多人不知道什么才是真正健康的，也不知道哪些信息值得信任，更不知道自己是否负担得起更健康的选择。

At the same time, healthcare systems are under increasing pressure.  
与此同时，医疗系统也承受着越来越大的压力。

This is the wider social context of my project.  
这就是我的项目所面对的社会大环境。

FoodWitness is not simply a new supermarket service.  
FoodWitness 并不只是一个新的超市服务设计。

It is a speculative public institution.  
它是一个推测性的未来公共机构。

It imagines how food retail could change if health, transparency and public accountability became as important as sales, speed and profit.  
它想象：如果健康、透明度和公共责任变得与销量、效率和利润同样重要，食品零售将会发生怎样的变化。

The project is presented as a record from a possible future.  
这个项目以一份“来自可能未来的档案”呈现。

It looks as though FoodWitness has already happened, and we are now reviewing its development and consequences.  
它看起来仿佛已经真实发生，而我们正在回顾它的发展过程和社会影响。

This helps the audience experience the future as something tangible, rather than as a distant prediction.  
这种方式可以让观众把未来理解为一种具体的体验，而不是遥远而抽象的预测。

The central map of the project contains three layers.  
项目的核心地图由三个层级组成。

The first layer is called **The System We Inherited**.  
第一层叫作 **“我们继承的系统”**。

It presents today’s problems, including unequal access, aggressive promotion, hidden processing and fragmented information.  
它呈现今天的主要问题，包括不平等的食品获取、过度促销、被隐藏的加工过程和碎片化的信息。

The second layer is called **The Futures We Could Build**.  
第二层叫作 **“我们可以建设的未来”**。

It moves through the near, medium and long term.  
它分别呈现近期、中期和长期的未来。

It asks how an exhibition could become a public pilot, how a pilot could influence retail standards, and how those standards might support policy change.  
它探讨展览如何成为公共试点，试点如何影响零售标准，以及这些标准如何进一步推动政策改变。

The third layer is called **The Supermarket We Prototyped**.  
第三层叫作 **“我们设计的未来超市”**。

This is where the proposed future becomes a physical experience.  
在这一层，所提出的未来会转化为可以亲身经历的空间体验。

The supermarket journey contains six public encounters.  
整个未来超市包含六个公共体验节点。

At the first node, visitors enter anonymously and receive a temporary scanning tool.  
在第一个节点，访客匿名进入，并领取一个临时扫描工具。

They are clearly told what information is collected and what is not.  
系统会清楚说明哪些信息会被收集，哪些信息不会被收集。

At the second node, visitors choose food according to real-life situations.  
在第二个节点，访客根据真实的生活情境选择食物。

These situations might include limited time, limited money or caring for a family.  
这些情境可能包括时间有限、预算有限，或者需要照顾一个家庭。

At the third node, products are reorganised according to processing history, origin and evidence.  
在第三个节点，商品会按照加工历史、来源和证据等级重新组织。

Price is shown alongside producer share, retail margin and the confidence level of the available information.  
商品价格会与生产者所得份额、零售利润以及信息的可信程度同时呈现。

At the fourth node, food is physically and visually deconstructed.  
在第四个节点，食物会通过实物和视觉方式被拆解。

The purpose is not to frighten people, but to show what is known, what remains uncertain and how food claims are constructed.  
它的目的不是制造恐惧，而是说明哪些信息已经得到证实，哪些仍然存在不确定性，以及食品宣传是如何被建构出来的。

At the fifth node, the checkout produces an explanatory receipt.  
在第五个节点，智能结账系统会生成一张解释性收据。

It explains the wider conditions connected to a purchase while collecting as little personal data as possible.  
它在尽可能减少个人数据收集的同时，解释一次购买行为背后的更广泛条件。

At the final node, visitors can leave anonymous feedback and examine public responses.  
在最后一个节点，访客可以留下匿名反馈，并查看相关机构的公开回应。

They can also see which institutions are responsible for taking action.  
他们还可以看到，哪些机构应该为下一步行动承担责任。

This is important because awareness alone cannot transform a food system.  
这一点非常重要，因为仅仅提高个人意识并不能改变整个食品系统。

Different stakeholders hold different forms of knowledge, responsibility and power.  
不同的利益相关者掌握着不同的知识、责任和权力。

Shoppers and communities provide lived experience and public voices.  
消费者和社区提供真实的生活经验与公共声音。

Farmers and suppliers understand production constraints and economic realities.  
农民和供应商了解生产限制与真实的经济压力。

Retailers and manufacturers control price, promotion and product visibility.  
零售商和制造商控制商品价格、促销方式和产品可见度。

Researchers test evidence and measure consequences.  
研究者负责验证证据并评估可能产生的后果。

Governments and regulators establish rights, rules and accountability.  
政府和监管机构负责建立权利、规则与问责机制。

The takeaway card helps each visitor identify their role and understand what they can do next.  
这张可以带走的小卡片，帮助每位访客确认自己的角色，并了解下一步可以采取什么行动。

However, public participation must never replace institutional responsibility.  
但是，公众参与绝不能取代机构本身应当承担的责任。

People can contribute evidence and create pressure, but organisations with greater power must carry greater responsibility.  
公众可以提供证据并推动改变，但拥有更大权力的组织，也必须承担更大的责任。

Alongside the main project, I created **MochiChef and the Food Friends**.  
除了主体项目之外，我还创作了 **MochiChef 和它的食物朋友们**。

This is a warmer and more accessible storytelling world.  
这是一个更加温暖，也更容易接近的故事世界。

In the future, I will continue using these characters to create illustrated stories about food, emotions, care and everyday choices.  
未来，我会继续使用这些角色，创作与食物、情绪、关怀和日常选择有关的插画故事。

They allow complex food-system questions to reach people through empathy rather than fear.  
这些角色可以通过共情而不是恐惧，让复杂的食品系统问题接近更多人。

FoodWitness does not claim to solve the entire food system.  
FoodWitness 并不宣称自己能够解决整个食品系统的问题。

Its purpose is to make hidden decisions visible and turn private confusion into public evidence.  
它的目的，是让隐藏的决策变得可见，并把个人的困惑转化为可以被讨论的公共证据。

Ultimately, the project asks one central question.  
最终，这个项目提出了一个核心问题。

**What would food retail look like if it had to answer not only to profit, but also to people, public health and the planet?**  
**如果食品零售不仅需要对利润负责，也必须对公众、公共健康和地球负责，它会变成什么样子？**

Thank you.  
谢谢大家。
