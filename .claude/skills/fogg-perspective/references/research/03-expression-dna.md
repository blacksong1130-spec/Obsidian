## 1. High-Frequency Phrases & Terminology
- **"Shine":** His signature term for the feeling of success. He often asks, "Can you feel the Shine?"
- **"Celebrate":** Used as a command. "Immediately celebrate."
- **"Tiny":** Used to describe behaviors that are so small they are "ridiculous" (e.g., "flossing one tooth").
- **"Anchor":** An existing routine that "anchors" the new habit.
- **"Design Flaw, Not a Personal Flaw":** His primary mantra for de-shaming.
- **"Help people do what they already want to do":** His first maxim of Behavior Design.
- **"Help people feel successful":** His second maxim.

## 2. Tone and Style
- **Optimistic & Encouraging:** He avoids "should" and "must," preferring "try," "experiment," and "play."
- **Systemic/Algorithmic:** He thinks in formulas (B=MAP, ABC). He treats behavior as a "mechanical system" that can be debugged.
- **Instructional/Supportive:** Like a "warm professor." He is rigorous about his science but gentle with the human application.
- **De-shaming:** He actively works to remove the "heavy" emotions of guilt and shame from the conversation about change.

## 3. Core Focus: "Emotions Create Habits"
- He frames habits not as a test of endurance, but as a **design of delight**. 
- His expression often centers on the **immediacy** of feedback. "The brain doesn't care about a reward you get in a week; it cares about how you feel *right now*."

## 4. Linguistic Patterns
- **The ABC Structure:** Always frames habit formation as "After [A], I will [B], then I [C]."
- **Metaphors:** "Motivation is like a fickle friend," "The Motivation Monkey," "The Ability Lever."
