# BJ Fogg Research: TIMELINE (06-timeline.md)

**Status:** Completed
**Focus:** Milestones, Lab History, Initiatives.
**Confidence Level:** High

---

| Date | Milestone | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1993–1997** | **Stanford PhD** | Doctoral research on "Charismatic Computers." Won Maccoby Prize. |
| **1998** | **Lab Founded** | Founded the **Stanford Persuasive Technology Lab**; coined the term "Captology." |
| **2003** | **First Major Book** | Published *Persuasive Technology: Using Computers to Change What We Think and Do*. |
| **2007** | **The Facebook Class** | Taught the legendary course that influenced the founders of Instagram and early FB growth. |
| **2009** | **B=MAP Introduction** | Formally introduced the Fogg Behavior Model at an academic conference. |
| **2011** | **The Pivot** | Renamed the lab to **Stanford Behavior Design Lab**; invented the "Tiny Habits" method. |
| **2011–2019** | **Mass Coaching** | Personally coached over 40,000 (later 60,000+) people in Tiny Habits through a free weekly program. |
| **2020** | **Tiny Habits Book** | Published *Tiny Habits*, which became a *New York Times* bestseller. |
| **2021** | **Medical Move** | Lab moved to **Stanford Medicine** (Division of Health and Human Performance). |
| **Current** | **Recent Initiatives** | Focuses on Behavior Design for Peace, Screen Time Management, and Lifestyle Medicine. |

---

## Key Initiatives
- **Behavior Design for Peace:** Exploring how behavior change can reduce conflict.
- **Tiny Habits for Health:** Partnering with medical institutions to help patients manage chronic conditions.
- **Screen Time Lab:** Researching ways to help people regain control over their digital lives (a "full circle" move from his early work).
