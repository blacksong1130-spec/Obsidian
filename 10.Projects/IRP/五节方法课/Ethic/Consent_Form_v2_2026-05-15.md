## Information for Participants

Dear Potential Participant,

I am a student in the **MDes Design Futures** programme at the **Royal College of Art**. As part of my Independent Research Project, I am conducting a study entitled **Why Eating Well Feels Like Nothing: Redesigning the Feedback Loop for Dietary Behaviour**. This project has secured ethical approval from the College.

This research explores how everyday food environments and bodily sensations after meals shape our relationship with food — and how design might help make eating well feel more natural, less pressured, and more in tune with what our bodies actually tell us.

**This study does not involve:**
- Any assessment of your weight, calorie intake, or diet quality
- Any judgement of what you eat or how you eat
- Any nutritional advice or recommendations

**Who should NOT participate:**
If you currently have a **clinical diagnosis of an eating disorder**, please do not take part in this study.

## What Participation Involves

Depending on which phase(s) you are invited to join, participation may include one or more of the following activities. The researcher will clearly explain which phase(s) apply to you before you sign this form.

---

**Phase 1 — Space Visit, Shared Meal & Conversation** *(approximately 90 minutes, individual)*

The researcher visits your everyday eating space (e.g., kitchen, dining area, or wherever you usually eat), shares a meal with you, and has a recorded conversation about your daily eating habits, body sensations after meals, and your experience with any food-related tools or apps you may have used. You can choose where the visit takes place and what you eat.

---

**Phase 2 — One-Day Photo Diary** *(same day, after each main meal)*

On one agreed day after Phase 1, within 15–25 minutes after each main meal, you:
1. Take a photo of your post-meal environment (the space around you — not the food itself)
2. Write 3 short sentences about how your body feels

You share these via a private photo album or direct message to the researcher. The diary takes approximately 10–15 minutes per meal.

---

**Phase 3 — Group Lunch Session** *(approximately 60 minutes)*

You bring your own lunch to a small group session (4–5 people) at RCA. The session involves:
- A brief written questionnaire before the meal (3 questions: hunger level, expectation, mood in one word)
- Eating lunch together (no structured activity during the meal)
- A facilitated group discussion after the meal about how the meal felt

> **Please note:** Other participants will be present in this session and may be able to recognise you. All participants are asked to treat each other's contributions as confidential and not to share what others say outside the session. The researcher will also not identify any individual in any reports arising from this study.

---

**Phase 4 — Design Conversation** *(approximately 40 minutes, individual)*

The researcher shows you printed photographs from your own photo diary (Phase 2) and two simple hand-drawn design concepts, and asks for your reactions and thoughts. There are no right or wrong answers. No rating scales or forms are used.

---

## Your Rights

- Participation is **entirely voluntary**. You may withdraw at any point before the project is submitted without giving any reason.
- You may choose to **opt out of specific recording formats** (audio, photo) without withdrawing from the study.
- All information you share will be **kept confidential**. You will not be identified in any reports, presentations, or publications arising from this study.
- Any images or quotes that could identify you will only be used in the public domain **with your express written permission**.
- All data will be **stored securely** and **destroyed** after the project is submitted.

If you have any questions or concerns about this project, please contact:
- **Shannon Larkin** (supervisor): Shannon.Larkin@rca.ac.uk
- **Taught Student Ethics Committee**: tse@rca.ac.uk

Thank you for your interest and your time.
