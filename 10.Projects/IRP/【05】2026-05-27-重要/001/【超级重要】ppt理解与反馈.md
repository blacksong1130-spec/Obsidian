**The widening gap** Our food system grows more complex every year. Our ability to read our own bodies does not.

**RQ:** How might design help people build a personal understanding of food — moving from external labels to their own body's knowledge?

_An awareness- and reflection-first inquiry — not a health-monitoring or optimisation system._

差距日益扩大 我们的食物系统每年都变得更加复杂，而我们了解自身身体的能力却没有同步提升。
研究问题：设计如何帮助人们建立对食物的个人理解——从外在标签转向自身身体的认知？
以认知和反思为先的探究——而非健康监测或优化系统。

**What the data revealed**

01 · "Eating healthy" produces no signal — effort with no feedback feels endless (Sisyphus). 02 · Knowing ≠ changing. Intention rarely survives the moment of choice. 03 · Ultra-processed food keeps raising the pleasure threshold. 04 · Tuning into the body takes time, money and calm — for many, that is a luxury.

**→ The pivot** From _studying food_ → to _studying the self_ From _"healthy vs. junk"_ → to _"no good or bad food, only food suited to my body and purpose right now"_ From _demanding body-awareness directly_ → to _a progressive scaffold: technique → awareness (术 → 道)_


数据揭示了什么
01 · “健康饮食”不会产生任何信号——没有反馈的努力让人感觉永无止境（西西弗斯）。
02 · 知道≠改变。意图很少能在选择的那一刻存续。
03 · 超加工食品不断提高愉悦阈值。
04 · 倾听身体的声音需要时间、金钱和冷静——对许多人来说，这是一种奢侈。
→ 研究重点从研究食物 → 转向研究自我从“健康与垃圾” → 转向“没有好坏食物，只有当下适合我身体和目标的食物”从直接要求身体觉知 → 转向渐进式支架：技法 → 觉知（术 → 道）
