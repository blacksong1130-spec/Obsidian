# IRP Tutorial Presentation · 10 min

> 格式：英文和中文内容完全对应，逐段互译。

---

## 1. The Problem
`body signals · disconnection · surveillance · anxiety · quit`

Most of us finish a meal without really knowing whether we ate well — not because we don't care, but because our connection to our own **body signals** has been drowned out by phones, emotions, and habit.
很多人吃完一顿饭，说不清自己是否真的吃得好。不是因为不在乎，而是因为我们和**身体信号**之间的**连接**，已经被手机、情绪和习惯填满了。

Existing tools — calorie counters, food trackers — don't fix this. They replace **body signals** with numbers, and numbers feel like **surveillance**, not support. People grow more **anxious**, and eventually **quit**.
现有的工具——卡路里计算器、饮食追踪 App——没有解决这个问题。它们把**身体信号**换成了数字，而数字让人感觉像**被监控**、被评判，而不是被支持。结果是：用的人越来越**焦虑**，最终**放弃**。

---

## 2. Research Question
`reconnect · no judgment · no numbers · environment design · sensory feedback`

My research question is: how can design help people **reconnect** with their body signals around eating — **without judgment, without numbers**?
我的研究问题是：**设计如何帮助人们在进食时重新连接自己的身体信号——不评判、不依赖数字？**

I'm approaching this through two lenses: **environment design**, which reduces friction and makes good choices easier; and **sensory feedback** design, which uses gentle prompts to invite body awareness rather than measure it.
我从两个维度来回答这个问题：**环境设计**——降低"吃好"的摩擦，让好的选择变容易；以及**感官反馈设计**——用温和的提示邀请身体感知，而不是测量它。

---

## 3. Three Things I Want to Know

Specifically, I have three research questions.
具体来说，我有三条核心研究问题。

---

**① Feedback acceptance threshold**
`care vs. surveillance · vibration · light · music · scent · Concept B`

For the same person, what is the difference between receiving a calorie count and receiving a warm light prompt? Across sensory types — **vibration**, **light**, **music**, **scent** — which feel like **care**, and which feel like **surveillance**? This boundary is the core data for **Concept B**.

**① 反馈接受阈值**
同一个人，对卡路里数字和一个暖色灯光提示，感受完全不同。在**震动**、**灯光**、**音乐**、**气味**这些感官形式里，哪种让人感到**被关怀**，哪种让人感到**被监控**？这条分界线是 **Concept B** 的核心设计依据。

---

**② Body signal vocabulary**
`language granularity · fullness words · perception · design gap`

What words do people actually use to describe **fullness**? "Stuffed," "just enough," "heavy," "light," "hollow" — each word points to a different **granularity of perception**. **The granularity of language determines the granularity of feeling.** This **vocabulary** doesn't exist in the literature, because those researchers don't design. I need it.

**② 身体信号的词汇表**
人们用什么词描述**饱感**？"撑""够了""舒服""重""空"——这些词背后是不同的**感知颗粒度**。**语言的颗粒度决定了感觉的颗粒度。** 文献里没有这个**词汇库**，因为那些研究者不做设计。我需要它，才能知道 Concept B 的提示语该用什么语言。

---

**③ Friction typology**
`disbelief · control · effort · three fixes · Concept A`

When participants resist changes to their eating environment, the reason matters. Is it **disbelief** that it works? A feeling of being **controlled**? Or simply too much **effort**? These **three types of resistance** require three completely different design responses — so I have to distinguish between them.

**③ 环境干预的阻力类型**
当参与者拒绝改变饮食空间，原因决定了解法。是**不相信它有效**？是**感觉被控制**？还是**太麻烦了**？这**三种阻力**对应三种完全不同的设计修正方向——所以我必须把它们区分开来。

---

## 4. Method — Four Phases

To answer these questions, I've designed four research phases over two to three weeks, with three to five participants.
为了回答这三个问题，我设计了四个调研阶段，总时长约2-3周，参与者3-5人。

---

**Phase 1 — Space observation + shared meal + interview · ~90 min**
`environmental audit · default grab points · spontaneous vocabulary · failure moments`

I first ask participants to show me their eating space — an **environmental audit** to see what food is visibly available, what's hidden, and where the **default grab points** are. Then we eat together, followed by a semi-structured interview. I'm collecting two things: the body sensation **vocabulary** they use **spontaneously**, and the type of resistance behind their past **failure moments**.

**第一阶段：空间快照 + 一顿饭 + 对话，约90分钟**
我先请参与者带我看他们的饮食空间——这是**环境审计**，看什么食物默认可见、什么被藏着、他们**随手抓的默认位置**在哪里。然后我们一起吃一顿饭，之后做半结构化访谈。主要收集两类数据：他们自发使用的**身体感受词汇**，以及**失败时刻**背后的**阻力类型**（不信 / 不爽 / 太烦）。

---

**Phase 2 — One-day photo diary · 15–25 min after each meal**
`20-min satiety window · photo + 3 lines · no researcher · authentic vocabulary`

Participants take a photo of the post-meal scene — not the food, but the empty plate, table, surroundings — and write **three lines**: where they ate, how their body feels, and whether that has shifted since finishing. The timing is grounded in Robinson et al. (2014): satiety signals take around **20 minutes** to travel from gut to brain. This phase captures the most **authentic vocabulary** — **no researcher present**, no prompting.

**第二阶段：一天照片日记，饭后15-25分钟内完成**
参与者拍一张饭后现场的照片（空盘子、桌面、周围环境），写**三行文字**：在哪里吃的、身体感觉怎样、和刚吃完相比有什么变化。时间窗口基于 Robinson et al. (2014)——饱感信号需要约 **20分钟** 从肠道传到大脑。这一阶段收集的是**自然情境下最真实的词汇数据**，**没有研究者在场**，没有引导。

---

**Phase 3 — Mindful lunch group · 4–5 people · ~60 min**
`social eating · group vocabulary · satiety window · Concept B first response`

Everyone brings their own lunch and eats together. The group discussion happens within the **20-minute satiety window**. I observe how **vocabulary** surfaces naturally in a **social eating** context, and whether the group setting affects how people express their body sensations. This also gives me an **initial response to Concept B**.

**第三阶段：正念午餐小组，4-5人，约60分钟**
大家各自带午饭，一起吃，然后在饭后 **20分钟的饱感窗口** 内进行小组讨论。我观察：**社交进食**情境下**词汇**如何自发浮现，以及群体环境是否影响人们表达身体感受的方式。同时收集对 **Concept B 的初步反应**。

---

**Phase 4 — Design probe interview · ~50 min · live sensory testing**
`diary as anchor · sensory spectrum · care vs. surveillance rating · friction diagnosis`

I open with the participant's own **diary photos** as anchor material, then present each sensory feedback type one by one — gradual warm light, vibration, a short music clip, scent — asking them to rate each as closer to **"care"** or **"surveillance."** I also present Concept A sketches and probe which type of **friction** they represent.

**第四阶段：设计探针访谈，约50分钟，含现场感官测试**
用参与者自己的**日记照片**作为开场锚点，然后逐一呈现不同感官反馈类型——灯光渐变、手机震动、音乐片段、气味——请他们评估每种形式更接近**"关怀"**还是**"监控"**。同时展示 Concept A（环境结构干预）草图，追问**阻力**来自哪里。

---

## 5. What the Data Gives Me
`3 datasets → 3 design decisions · spectrum · vocabulary · friction map`

These four phases produce **three datasets** that map directly onto design decisions: a **sensory feedback spectrum** to determine what to build for Concept B; a **body vocabulary archive** to determine what language the interface should speak; and a **friction typology** to determine which aspect of Concept A to address first.
四个阶段产出**三份数据**，直接对应三个设计决策：**感官反馈光谱**（决定 Concept B 用哪种感官形式）、**身体词汇档案**（决定提示语用什么语言）、**阻力类型图谱**（决定 Concept A 优先修正哪个方向）。

---

## 6. Closing
`not a tracker · interface · their language · their senses`

I'm not building a better **tracker**. I'm designing an **interface** between people and their own bodies — in the **language** they already use, through the **senses** they already have.
我不是在做一个更好的**追踪工具**。我想做的是：**给人们一个和自己身体对话的界面**——用他们自己的**语言**，通过他们已经拥有的**感官**。

## Fogg 逻辑校验（备注，不用讲）

> - **Motivation**：招募标准已筛选有动力的人。不需要创造，只需要不摧毁。
> - **Ability**：Phase 1 审计物理环境；词汇研究本身测量的是内感受的认知能力。
> - **Prompt**：Phase 4 直接测试哪种 Prompt 有效、哪种让人逃跑。
>
> 逻辑链：问题 → 框架 → 三条研究问题 → 四个阶段 → 三份数据 → 设计原则。每步从上一步推出。
