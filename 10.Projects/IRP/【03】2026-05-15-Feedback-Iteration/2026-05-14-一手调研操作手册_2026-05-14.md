# Primary Research Protocol / 一手调研完整操作手册
> Step-by-step instructions with real-world references / 每一步具体怎么做 + 别人是怎么做的

---

## 🗺️ Global Flow / 全局流程图

```
Step 0: Preparation & Recruitment 
第0步：准备材料 & 招募参与者（本周）
    ↓
Step 1: Day in the Life Observation (accompany 2 meals)（observe the behaviour）
第1步：Day in the Life 观察（跟两顿饭）
    ↓
Step 2: Semi-structured Interview (same day, after observation)（ask the feeling about the insight i found）
第2步：半结构化访谈（同一天，观察后）
    ↓
Step 3: 3-Day Photo Diary (participant-led)——（eat alone）
第3步：3天照片日记（参与者独立完成）
    ↓
Step 4: Mindful Lunch Group (4–5 people, one session)——（eat with others）（watch every think moment to make decision，eg：eat what）
第4步：正念午餐小组（4-5人，一次性）
    ↓
Step 5: Autoethnography (researcher's parallel reflective journal)（think the behaviour change）
第5步：自我民族志（研究者平行反思日记）
    ↓
Step 6: Design Probe Follow-up Interview (after diary)（test feedback）
第6步：设计探针反应访谈（日记结束后）
    ↓
Step 7: Data Analysis (Reflexive Thematic Analysis)
第7步：数据分析（主题归纳）
```

## Step 0: Preparation & Recruitment / 第0步：准备 & 招募（做调研之前）

### Materials Checklist / 你需要准备的材料清单

**Participants need a photo diary instruction card (A5, printed or sent as image):**

**给参与者的日记指令卡（A5大小，打印或发图片）：**
```
Post-Meal Photo Diary Instructions / 餐后照片日记说明

Over the next 3 days, within 60 seconds after each main meal:
接下来3天，每顿正餐结束后请在60秒内：

① Take one photo of the "post-meal scene"
  拍一张"饭后现场"照片
   Include: empty plates, table surface, hands, surrounding environment
   拍：空盘子、桌面、双手、周围环境
   Exclude: the food itself
   不要拍：食物本身

② Write 3 lines in your phone notes (no numbers needed):
  用手机备忘录写3行（不用数字）：
   · Where did you eat? What were you doing at the same time?
     我在哪里吃的？同时在做什么？
   · How does your body feel right now?
     我的身体现在感觉怎样？
     (words like: heavy / light / full / warm / calm / anxious / satisfied / empty…)
     （可以用：沉/轻/胀/暖/平静/焦虑/满足/空/安定……）
   · Did this meal feel okay? Why?
     这顿饭感觉还好吗？为什么？

Send to our shared folder / WeChat me directly.
发到我们的共享文件夹 / 直接发微信给我
Thank you! / 谢谢！
```

**Tools you will need:**

**你需要的工具：**
- Recording device (phone voice memo works fine for interviews) / 录音设备（手机备忘录录音就行，访谈用）
- Signed consent form (required by Ethics) / 知情同意书（Ethics要求，见下）
- Notebook for observation notes / 笔记本记录观察
- Printed or digital instruction card / 打印版或电子版指令卡

### Participant Information & Consent / 知情同意书（Ethics必须）

Your IRP involves human participants — written informed consent is mandatory before any data collection. Without it, the data cannot be used in your report.

你的 IRP 涉及人类参与者，**必须有书面知情同意**，否则数据不能用于报告。

**The consent form must cover:**

**同意书需要包含：**
- Purpose of the research (one sentence) / 研究目的（一句话）
- What participants will do (time/activities) / 参与者需要做什么（时间/活动）
- How data will be used (anonymised) / 数据如何使用（匿名化）
- Right to withdraw at any time / 随时可以退出
- Contact details / 联系方式

**Template reference:**

**参考模板：**
→ RCA Ethics Instructions（Canvas课程页面上有这个PDF，直接下载用）

**How others have done it:**

**别人怎么写的：**
→ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10537183/
（这篇饮食情境日记研究，Methods部分有完整的伦理审批说明，可以看他们怎么描述的）

### Recruitment / 招募参与者

**Who to recruit:**

**找谁：**
- RCA students (easiest to recruit) / RCA同学（最容易招募）
- People who have experience tracking their diet with an app / 有过用app追踪饮食经历的人
- People who have tried but failed to change their eating habits / 有过尝试改变饮食但失败的人
- **Exclude:** anyone currently diagnosed with an eating disorder / **排除**：目前有饮食障碍诊断的人

**Message template to send:**

**怎么说（发消息模板）：**
```
Hi! I'm doing IRP research on the relationship between food tracking and body awareness.
嗨！我在做IRP调研，关于食物追踪和身体感受的关系。
What I need from you:
需要你做：
- Join me for two meals (I just observe, no judgement)
  跟我一起吃两顿饭（我只是在旁边观察）
- Keep a 3-day post-meal photo diary (5 min each time, send me photos)
  之后做3天饭后日记（每次5分钟，发照片给我）
- Chat for 30 minutes at the end
  最后聊30分钟

Fully anonymous, nothing about what/how much you eat.
全程匿名，不涉及你吃什么/体重/卡路里。
Interested?
有兴趣吗？
```

**How many participants:**

**需要多少人：**
- Day in the Life + interview + diary: 2–3 people (in-depth) / 2-3人（深度）
- Mindful lunch group: 4–5 people (one session, can overlap with above) / 正念午餐小组：4-5人（一次性，可以就是你的同学）
- These two groups can be different people / 两类可以是不同的人

## Step 1: Day in the Life Observation / 第1步：Day in the Life 观察

### How to do it / 具体怎么做

This step involves accompanying 2–3 participants through two meals in a single day (e.g., lunch + dinner, or breakfast + lunch). Your role is that of a quiet, non-judgemental observer — present but not intervening.

陪同参与者经历同一天的两顿饭（如午饭+晚饭，或早饭+午饭）。你的角色是安静的观察者，不评论，不建议，就是在场。

**Time:** Two meals in the same day / **时间：** 同一天两顿饭

**Your role:** Silent observer — no comments, no advice, just be present / **你的角色：** 安静的观察者，不评论，不建议，就是在场

**What to observe (record in notebook, do not say aloud):**

**观察什么（用笔记本记，不用说出来）：**

```
Observation Record / 观察记录表

Time: ____  Location: ____  /  时间：____  地点：____
Participant code: P1/P2/P3  /  参与者代号：P1/P2/P3

[Before eating / 饭前]
□ Where did the food come from? (cooked/takeaway/supermarket/canteen)
  食物从哪里来的？（自己做/外卖/超市/食堂）
□ How did they decide what to eat? (planned/spontaneous/checked fridge)
  怎么决定吃什么的？（提前计划/随机/看冰箱）
□ Emotional state (observe, do not ask):
  情绪状态（观察，不要问）：

[During eating / 进食中]
□ What are they doing while eating? (phone/TV/talking/nothing)
  吃饭时在做什么？（刷手机/看电视/聊天/什么都不做）
□ Pace: fast / medium / slow  /  吃饭速度：快/中/慢
□ Did they pause at any point?  /  有没有中途停下来过？

[After eating / 饭后]
□ First action after finishing?  /  吃完后第一个动作是什么？
□ How long before they left the table?  /  多久后离开饭桌？
□ Did they say anything about the meal?  /  有没有对这顿饭说什么？

[Environment / 环境]
□ What was the eating environment like? (light/noise/tidiness)
  吃饭的地方是什么样的？（光线/声音/整洁程度）
□ How was the food laid out? (casual/intentional arrangement)
  食物是怎么摆放的？（随手放/有意识摆放）
```

**What NOT to do:**

**不要做的事：**
- Do not ask "Did you think that meal was healthy?" / 不要问"你觉得这顿饭健康吗"
- Do not react with any expression to their choices / 不要对他们的选择做任何表情反应
- Do not take photos (unless explicitly consented) / 不要拍照（除非征得同意）

**How others have done it:**

**别人怎么做的：**
→ https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2021.651749/full
（用Day in the Life + 数字日记研究暴食症日常体验，Methods部分描述观察协议）

→ https://think.design/user-design-research/a-day-in-the-life/
（方法解释 + 为什么适合捕捉习惯性行为的好参考）

## Step 2: Semi-Structured Interview / 第2步：半结构化访谈

Conducted on the same day as observation, after both meals. Duration: 45–60 minutes. Record with phone voice memo (with consent) and take written notes.

在观察的同一天，两顿饭之后单独约。**时长：** 45-60分钟。**工具：** 手机录音（征得同意），笔记本。

### Full Interview Script / 完整访谈问题逐字稿

**Opening (not recorded) / 开场（不录音）：**
> "Thank you for letting me join your meals today. I'd like to have a chat — I won't ask anything too personal, there are no right or wrong answers, I just want to understand your real experience. I'll record the audio, but only I will hear it, and it will be anonymised in the report. Is that okay?"

> "谢谢你今天让我陪着你吃饭。我想先聊聊，不会问很私密的东西，没有对错，我只是想了解你真实的体验。我会录音，但只有我一个人听，论文里会匿名处理。可以吗？"

**Warm-up (5 min) / 热身（5分钟）：**
> "Can you walk me through what a typical day of eating looks like for you — what's planned, what's spontaneous?"

> "帮我描述一下你平时一天的饮食大概是怎样的——哪些是计划好的，哪些是随机发生的？"

**Moments of failure (15 min) — the most important section / 失败时刻（15分钟）——这部分最重要：**
> "Think of a time when you seriously tried to change your eating but couldn't stick with it. What specifically made it hard — too effortful? Felt meaningless? Something else?"

> "想一个你曾经认真尝试改变饮食但没能坚持的时候。具体是什么让它变难了——太费劲？感觉没意义？还是别的？"

> "When do you eat 'on autopilot', without really thinking? What triggers it — a place, an emotion, a time of day?"

> "你什么时候会'不经大脑地吃东西'？是什么触发的——某个地点、情绪、还是某个时间点？"

> "If you could change one thing about your eating environment to make eating well easier, what would it be?"

> "如果可以改变你吃饭环境里的一件事，让吃得好变容易，会是什么？"

**Body signals (15 min) — core data / 身体信号（15分钟）——核心数据：**
> "When a meal feels 'right', how do you know? What does your body feel like ten minutes after?"

> "当一顿饭感觉'对了'，你怎么知道？饭后十分钟你的身体具体是什么感觉？"

> "Have you ever finished a meal and been unsure whether you'd eaten well? What does that uncertainty feel like?"

> "你有没有吃完饭后不确定自己吃得好不好？那种不确定是什么感觉？"

> "Can you remember a moment when you clearly felt 'enough'? What brought that feeling?"

> "你能记得一个你清楚感受到'够了'的时刻吗？是什么带来了那种感觉？"

**Tracking tools (10 min) / 追踪工具（10分钟）：**
> "Have you ever used an app or another tool to track your eating? What did those numbers actually do to you?"

> "你用过app或其他工具记录饮食吗？那些数字对你实际上做了什么？"

> "When did tracking feel like support, and when did it feel like judgement?"

> "追踪什么时候感觉像支持，什么时候感觉像评判？"

> "If something could tell you how a meal went — without numbers — what feeling or information would you want?"

> "如果有东西能告诉你这顿饭怎么样——不用数字——你想要什么样的感受或信息？"

**Closing / 结尾：**
> "Is there anything I haven't asked that you think is relevant?"

> "有什么我没问到的，你觉得和这个话题有关的吗？"

### Interview Notes / 访谈注意事项

- **Don't rush to fill silence:** if the participant pauses, wait 5 seconds — they usually say something more honest. / **沉默不要急着填**：如果参与者停顿了，等5秒，他们通常会说出更真实的东西
- **Follow up with "can you say more about that?"** Never use "so do you mean…" (this leads the answer). / **追问用"能说说更多吗？"** 不要用"你是说……吗"（会引导答案）
- **Don't react with surprise or judgement to any answer.** / **不要对任何回答表示惊讶或评价**

**How others have done it:**

**别人怎么做的：**
→ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6360478/
（直觉饮食质性研究，11名参与者，有完整的访谈问题设计和分析框架）

→ https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0278979

## Step 3: 3-Day Photo Diary / 第3步：3天照片日记

Participants complete this independently. Your job is to distribute the instruction card, send gentle reminders, and collect the materials.

**参与者独立完成，你只需要定时收材料。**

### Your tasks / 你的工作

**Day 0 (send out):** Send participants the instruction card and make sure they understand it. / **Day 0（发出）：** 把指令卡发给参与者，说明清楚

**Days 1–3:** Send a short daily reminder each evening if they forget. / **Day 1-3：** 每天晚上给参与者发一条简短提醒（如果他们忘了）

**Day 3 (collect):** Collect all photos and written entries. / **Day 3（收回）：** 收集所有照片 + 文字记录

**How to collect:**

**怎么收：**
- Set up a shared album (iPhone / Google Photos) / 建一个共享相册（iPhone/Google Photos）
- Or ask them to send via WeChat / 或者直接微信发给你

**What to do after collecting:**

**收到后你要做的：**
- Print each participant's photos in chronological order (A4, 4 per page) / 把每个人的照片按时间序打印出来（A4，4张/页）
- Write their text notes next to each photo / 在每张照片旁边写上他们的文字记录
- This becomes the opening material for the Step 5 design probe interview / 这就是第5步探针访谈的"开场材料"

**How others have done it:**

**别人怎么做的：**
→ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6371351/
（OneNote Meal 照片日记研究，有完整的指令设计和数据收集方法）

→ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10952333/
（饮食障碍恢复者的日记研究，用app收集日记，看他们怎么处理即时数据）

## Step 4: Mindful Lunch Group / 第4步：正念午餐小组

**One session, 4–5 participants, 45–60 minutes.**

**一次性活动，4-5人，45-60分钟。**

### Detailed Schedule / 详细流程

**Preparation / 准备：**
- Location: a school public space or studio with a table / 地点：学校公共空间/工作室，有桌子
- Ask everyone to bring their own lunch / 通知大家"带自己的午饭来"
- Prepare: 3 brief questionnaires (pre/during/post-meal) + phone recorder / 准备：3个简短问卷（饭前/饭中/饭后）+ 手机录音

**Timeline / 流程时间表：**

```
0:00–0:05  Arrive, brief introduction to the research (keep it short — don't prime them)
           到齐，简单介绍研究背景（不要说太多，不要影响他们）

0:05–0:10  Pre-meal questionnaire (3 questions, written on paper):
           饭前问卷（3个问题，写在纸上）：
           · How hungry are you? (0–10)  /  你现在饿吗？（0-10分）
           · Are you looking forward to this meal?  /  你期待这顿饭吗？
           · How are you feeling today? (one word)  /  你今天感觉怎样？（一个词）

0:10–0:30  Eat together (normal social interaction, do not intervene)
           一起吃饭（正常社交，你不干预）
           Observe: who eats fast/slow, who uses phone, who monitors what they eat
           你在观察：谁吃得快/慢，谁边吃边看手机，谁在注意自己吃了什么

0:30–0:50  Post-meal interview (critical time window — see below)
           饭后即时访谈（关键时间窗口）

0:50–1:00  Optional: show Concept B sketch, gather first reactions
           可选：展示Concept B草图，听第一反应
```

**Post-meal discussion questions (answer in turn, no judgement) / 饭后访谈问题（轮流回答，不评判彼此）：**

> "Now that the meal is over — how does your body feel? Describe it in a few words."

> "现在饭吃完了，你的身体感觉怎样？用几个词描述。"

> "Was there a moment during the meal when you felt 'enough'? Or did you just eat until the plate was empty?"

> "这顿饭有没有一个你感受到'够了'的时刻？还是一直吃到盘子空了？"

> "If a prompt arrived 20 minutes after eating, asking you to pause and notice your body — no score, no judgement — would that feel useful or annoying?"

> "如果饭后20分钟有个提示让你停下来感受一下身体——没有评分——你觉得有用还是烦？"

> "Do you usually find yourself thinking about whether you 'ate well' after a meal?"

> "你平时吃完饭会想到自己'吃得好不好'这件事吗？"

**Why 20 minutes matters:**

**为什么20分钟很重要：**

Robinson et al. (2014) demonstrated that satiety signals take approximately 20 minutes to travel from the gut to the brain. The group session takes place exactly within this window — this is not incidental, it is a deliberate design choice. This should be written into your report.

Robinson et al. (2014) 证明饱感信号需要约20分钟从肠道传到大脑。你的小组恰好在这个窗口里做访谈——这不是巧合，这就是你的研究方法的设计逻辑。在报告里要写出来。

**How others have done it:**

**别人怎么做的：**
→ https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0278979
（3个焦点小组，23人，讨论直觉饮食——有完整的问题设计和分析框架）

→ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4801689/
（正念饮食干预的完整协议，包括主持人角色/参与者任务/每节内容）

## Step 5: Design Probe Follow-up Interview / 第5步：设计探针反应访谈

**Conducted after the diary period, individually with each participant. Duration: 30–40 minutes.**

**在日记结束后，每个参与者单独约，30-40分钟。**

### Structure / 流程

**1. Open with their own diary materials (10 min) / 用他们自己的日记开场（10分钟）**

Lay their printed photos on the table.

把他们的照片打印出来放在桌上：

> "I printed out your photos from the 3 days. Let's look at them together — what do you feel when you see these?"

> "我把你这3天的照片打印出来了。我们一起看看——你看到这些照片，有什么感觉？"

> "Which photo feels most different from how your body actually felt at that moment?"

> "哪张照片和你当时的身体感受最不一样？"

**2. Show Concept A (10 min) / 展示 Concept A（10分钟）**

Show a simple sketch: a bowl divided into sections for vegetables, protein, and carbohydrates.

展示一个简单草图：碗里食物分区摆放（蔬菜/蛋白质/碳水分开）。

> "If your tableware was designed this way — automatically separating the food — do you think it would change how you eat?"

> "如果你的餐具是这样设计的——食物会自动分开摆放——你觉得这会改变你怎么吃吗？"

> "Does this feel like help, or does it feel like control?"

> "这感觉像帮助，还是感觉被控制？"

**3. Show Concept B (10 min) / 展示 Concept B（10分钟）**

Show a sketch: a gentle light/vibration 20 minutes after eating, inviting body awareness with no score.

展示草图：饭后20分钟，一个简单的灯光/震动，邀请你感受身体，无评分。

> "Twenty minutes after a meal, a gentle prompt invites you to pause and notice your body — no score, no judgement. Would you stop and check in? Or would you ignore it?"

> "饭后20分钟，一个温和的提示邀请你停下来感受身体，没有分数，没有评判。你会停下来吗？还是会忽略它？"

> "Does this feel like care, or surveillance?"

> "这感觉像关怀还是监控？"

**4. Open questions (10 min) / 开放问题（10分钟）**

> "If you could design something to help your relationship with food, what would you want it to do?"

> "如果可以设计一个东西帮助你和食物的关系更好，你希望它做什么？"

> "What is something you would absolutely not want?"

> "什么是你绝对不想要的？"

## Step 6: Autoethnography / 第6步：自我民族志

Autoethnography treats the researcher's own lived experience as a legitimate data source. Running parallel to all other research methods, you keep a personal reflective journal throughout the entire research period. Unlike participant diaries, this is your own ongoing record — positioning yourself as both researcher and subject.

自我民族志将研究者自身的生活经验作为合法的数据来源。在整个研究期间，你与其他所有方法同步保持一本个人反思日记，贯穿始终。与参与者日记不同，这是你自己持续的记录——将你同时定位为研究者和研究对象。

### What to record / 记录什么

**After each meal (5 minutes, same format as participant diary):**

**每顿饭后（5分钟，与参与者日记格式相同）：**
- Where did you eat? What were you doing at the same time? / 在哪里吃的？同时在做什么？
- How does your body feel right now? (use descriptive words, not numbers) / 身体现在感觉怎样？（用描述性词语，不用数字）
- Did this meal feel okay? Why? / 这顿饭感觉还好吗？为什么？

**After each research session (10 minutes):**

**每次做完调研后（10分钟）：**
- What surprised you about a participant's response? / 参与者的什么回答让你意外？
- Where did your own experience align with or diverge from theirs? / 你自己的经验和他们有什么相同或不同的地方？
- What assumptions did you bring in, and did the session challenge them? / 你带入了哪些预设？这次调研有没有挑战它们？

**Researcher positionality note (once, at the beginning):**

**研究者立场说明（开始时写一次）：**
Write a short paragraph about your own relationship with food tracking, dietary apps, and body awareness — this becomes the foundation of your reflexivity statement in the final report.

写一段话，记录你自己与食物追踪、饮食应用、身体感知的关系——这将成为最终报告中反思性声明的基础。

### Why this matters for ethics / 为什么这对伦理很重要

Autoethnography involving only the researcher does not require participant consent. However, it must be clearly labelled as researcher data in the report, separate from participant data. If any third parties are mentioned in your reflective entries, they should be anonymised.

仅涉及研究者本人的自我民族志不需要参与者同意。但在报告中，它必须被明确标注为研究者数据，与参与者数据分开处理。如果反思日记中提到任何第三方，应对其进行匿名化处理。

→ Ellis, C., Adams, T.E., & Bochner, A.P. (2011). Autoethnography: An overview. *Forum: Qualitative Social Research*, 12(1).

## Step 7: Data Analysis / 第7步：数据分析

All data — interviews, photo diaries, group discussion, and autoethnographic journal — are analysed together using Reflexive Thematic Analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006/2022).

所有数据——访谈、照片日记、小组讨论、自我民族志日记——统一使用 **Reflexive Thematic Analysis（Braun & Clarke 2006/2022）** 进行分析。

### Step-by-step / 具体步骤

**Step 1: Organise raw materials / 整理原始材料**

- Interview recordings → transcribe using Otter.ai or Whisper / 访谈录音 → 用 Otter.ai 或 Whisper 转录成文字
- Photo diary → print as a grid, write text notes beside each photo / 照片日记 → 打印成网格，每张旁边写文字描述
- Observation notes → scan or type up / 观察笔记 → 扫描或整理成文档
- Autoethnographic journal → treat as a separate strand of data / 自我民族志日记 → 作为单独的数据链处理

**Step 2: First-pass coding (find everything interesting) / 第一轮编码（找所有有意思的点）**

Read through all materials. Mark anything that catches your attention — the notes are descriptive at this stage: "participant eating while scrolling phone" / "uses the word 'heavy' to describe feeling full." Do not look for themes yet.

通读所有材料，在任何引起你注意的地方做标注。标注是描述性的。此阶段不要找主题，先只是标注。

**Step 3: Cluster into themes / 归类成主题**

Group similar codes together and name each cluster:
把相似的标注归在一起，给每一组起一个名字：
- e.g. "Autopilot eating triggers" / 例如："自动驾驶进食触发因素"
- e.g. "Language of body signals" (what words people use to describe fullness) / 例如："身体信号语言"（人们用哪些词描述饱感）
- e.g. "The paradox of tracking" (support vs. judgement) / 例如："追踪的矛盾感"（帮助 vs 评判）

**Step 4: Answer the research question with themes / 用主题回答研究问题**

Your research question concerns how speculative design might help rebuild body awareness. Which themes support Concept A/B? Which challenge them?

你的研究问题是关于"投机设计如何帮助重建身体感知"——哪些主题支持你的 Concept A/B？哪些主题挑战了它？
