# IRP Research Plan · 双语研究计划
**7 May 2026 · For Supervisor Discussion · 导师讨论用**

---

## 1. Core Research Question · 核心研究问题

How can environmental and contextual design help people read their own body signals more accurately —
怎么通过环境和情境设计，帮助人更准确地读懂自己身体的信号——

and provide non-judgmental external reference when those signals are unclear?
同时在信号不清晰时，提供不评判的外部参照？

---

## 2. Target User · 目标用户

The target user is an adult who wants to lose weight or eat more healthily, but consistently struggles.
目标用户是一个想减肥或吃得更健康、但总是失败的成年人。

They don't know what to eat, tend to restrict (starving themselves), and feel uncertain after eating: "was that okay?"
她不知道该吃什么，倾向于节食忍饿，吃完之后感到不确定："这顿吃得对吗？"

They need reassurance that they are on the right path.
她需要一种"我在正确的道路上"的确定感。

This includes both people who cook at home and people who primarily eat takeout.
这包括自己做饭的人，也包括主要点外卖的人。

**Core insight:** This person does not need more nutritional information.
**核心洞察：** 这个人不是缺乏营养知识。

The real problem is broken trust in her own body's signals — she has outsourced food decisions to external rules and data.
真正的问题是对自身身体信号的信任断裂——她把本来应该在身体里的判断，外包给了规则、App和数字。

---

## 3. Theoretical Framework · 理论框架

This project uses Fogg's Behaviour Model (B = MAP), focusing on two components: Ability and Motivation.
本项目基于Fogg行为模型（B=MAP），聚焦其中两个部分：能力（A）和动机（M）。

**Ability (A) — Environmental Design:**
**能力（A）——环境设计：**

Reduce friction for healthy choices by changing the physical context, so that good choices become the default without requiring willpower.
通过改变物理环境减少健康选择的阻力，让好的选择成为默认状态，不依赖意志力。

**Motivation (M) — Non-punitive Feedback:**
**动机（M）——非惩罚性反馈：**

When body signals are unclear, provide a gentle external reference — not a score, not a judgement, but a prompt to notice what the body is experiencing right now.
当身体信号不够清晰时，提供温和的外部参照——不是打分，不是评判，而是一个让你注意自己当下身体状态的提示。

**Key position on body signals:**
**关于身体信号的关键立场：**

Body signals can be misleading: satiety takes 20 minutes to reach the brain;
身体信号有时会骗人：饱腹感需要20分钟才能到达大脑；

ultra-processed foods are engineered to bypass satiety mechanisms;
超加工食品被专门设计来绕过饱足信号；

chronic dieters often cannot distinguish true hunger from emotional eating.
长期节食者往往无法区分真正的饥饿和情绪性进食。

Therefore, the goal is not "trust your body completely."
因此，目标不是"完全信任身体"。

It is: design conditions where body signals become clearer, with gentle support when they remain ambiguous.
而是：设计让身体信号变得更清晰的条件，在信号仍然模糊时提供温和支持。

## 4. Secondary Research · 二手调研

### What is already in the paper · 现有文献（保留）

- Fogg (2009) — Behaviour Model B=MAP · 行为模型
- Kahneman (2011) — Present bias · 现时偏差
- Tribole & Resch (2020) — Intuitive Eating · 直觉饮食
- Roth et al. (2024) — Self-monitoring and eating disorder risk · 追踪与饮食障碍风险
- Zeevi et al. (2015) — Bioindividuality in glycaemic response · 血糖个体差异
- Thaler & Sunstein (2008) — Nudge · 助推理论

### What needs to be added · 需要补充的文献

**① Eating order · 进食顺序**
Sun et al. (2020) PATTERN study:
Eating vegetables and protein before carbohydrates significantly reduces the postprandial blood glucose peak.
先吃蔬菜和蛋白质再吃碳水化合物，显著降低餐后血糖峰值。

**② Plate and vessel perception · 餐具与视觉错觉**
Van Ittersum & Wansink (2012):
Smaller plates exploit the Delboeuf illusion — the same portion looks larger, and people naturally serve and eat less.
小盘子利用Delboeuf视觉错觉——相同分量看起来更多，人们自然少盛少吃。

**③ Food visibility and accessibility · 食物可见性与可及性**
Painter, Wansink & Hieggelke (2002):
Placing healthy food in the most visible and accessible position reliably shifts food choices without conscious effort.
把健康食物放在最显眼、最容易拿到的位置，能有效改变选择，不需要有意识的努力。

**④ Eating pace and satiety · 进食速度与饱腹感**
Robinson et al. (2014) meta-analysis:
Eating more slowly gives gut-brain satiety hormones time to signal fullness, reducing overall energy intake.
慢速进食给肠道-大脑饱腹激素时间传递信号，自然减少总摄入量。

**⑤ Food arrangement and visual separation · 食物排列与视觉分离**
Szocs & Lefebvre (2017):
Spreading food horizontally rather than stacking it increases perceived volume — the brain anticipates greater satiety.
食物横向分开摆放比堆叠感知体积更大，大脑预期更强的饱腹感。

**⑥ Micro-environmental design · 微环境设计**
Marteau & Hollands (2015) Cochrane review:
Changing food placement and portion vessel size is a low-cost, high-effect behavioural intervention.
改变食物摆放位置和容器大小是低成本、高效果的行为干预。

**⑦ Cultural food environments · 文化饮食环境**
Rozin et al. (2003):
French people eat less not through willpower but through structurally smaller physical portions and environments.
法国人吃少不靠意志力，靠的是物理上更小的分量和饮食环境结构。

## 5. Primary Research Plan · 一手调研计划

### Participants · 参与者

6–8 adults aged 22–45, with experience of trying to change their eating habits (regardless of success).
6-8名22-45岁的成年人，有过尝试改变饮食习惯的经历（不论成功与否）。

Prior use of any dietary tracking tool is preferred (app, notebook, meal plan).
有过任何形式饮食追踪经历者优先（App、记录本、饮食计划等）。

Exclusion: current clinical eating disorder diagnosis.
排除：当前临床饮食障碍诊断。

---

### Phase 1: Kitchen Walkthrough + Semi-Structured Interview · 阶段一：厨房导览 + 半结构访谈
**60–75 minutes · 60-75分钟**

#### Kitchen Walkthrough (first 15 minutes) · 厨房导览（前15分钟）

Ask the participant to show you their kitchen or main eating space — in person or via video call.
让参与者带你参观他们的厨房或主要进食空间——当面或视频通话均可。

*Questions · 问题：*

"Open your fridge for me — how is everything arranged?"
"打开你的冰箱给我看看——东西是怎么放的？"

"Where do you keep snacks? Where are the vegetables?"
"你的零食放在哪里？蔬菜在哪？"

"What's the first thing you see when you open the fridge?"
"打开冰箱第一眼你看到的是什么？"

"Where do you usually grab food from when you're not really thinking?"
"你最常从哪里拿东西吃？"

**Why:** Environmental design research must look at real environments.
**为什么：** 环境设计研究必须看真实的环境。

People's descriptions often differ from reality — the walkthrough shows what is actually hidden and what is visible.
人们的描述往往和实际情况不同——导览会告诉你什么真正被藏起来，什么在显眼位置。

#### Interview (following 45 minutes) · 访谈（后45分钟）

**Finding failure moments · 找失败时刻**

"Think of a time you tried to change how you eat but couldn't sustain it. What specifically made it difficult?"
"有没有一次你认真想改变饮食，但坚持不下去？具体是什么让你放弃了？"

"When do you eat on autopilot? What tends to trigger that — a place, a feeling, a time of day?"
"你什么时候会不思考就吃？什么触发了那个时刻——某个地方、某种情绪、某个时间点？"

"If you could change one physical thing in your kitchen to make healthy eating easier — without willpower — what would it be?"
"如果不靠意志力，你愿意改变厨房里一件物理上的事让健康饮食更容易——你会改什么？"

**Exploring body signals · 探索身体信号**

"When you finish a meal that feels 'right', how do you know? What does your body feel like ten minutes later?"
"吃完一顿感觉'还不错'的饭，你怎么知道？10分钟后你的身体是什么感觉？"

"Have you ever finished a meal feeling uncertain — not sure if it was okay? What does that feel like?"
"有没有吃完之后感到不确定的时候——不知道这顿吃得好不好？那种感觉是什么？"

**Tracking tool experience · 追踪工具经历**

"Have you used an app or tool to track what you eat? When did it feel like support, and when did it feel like judgement?"
"你用过App或工具追踪饮食吗？什么时候它感觉像支持，什么时候感觉像审判？"

---

### Phase 2: 3-Day Post-Meal Photo Diary · 阶段二：3天饭后照片日记
**Conducted after Phase 1 · 在访谈后进行**

After each main meal, within 60 seconds, participants complete three things:
每次吃完主餐，60秒内完成三件事：

**① Take one photo — not of the food, but of the aftermath:**
**① 拍一张照片——不是食物，是吃完之后的场景：**

the empty plate, the table, the surroundings, their hands.
空盘子、桌子、周围环境、自己的手。

**② Write three lines:**
**② 写三行字：**

*Where am I eating? What else am I doing right now?*
*我在哪里吃的？当时还在做什么？*

*What does my body feel like right now? (Words only — no numbers: heavy, light, warm, bloated, calm, anxious...)*
*吃完身体是什么感觉？（用形容词，不用数字：沉、轻、暖、胀、平静、焦虑……）*

*Did this meal feel okay? Why, or why not?*
*这顿饭有没有让我觉得"还不错"？为什么有，或者为什么没有？*

**Why:** This captures real eating moments, not retrospective memories.
**为什么：** 这捕捉的是真实的进食时刻，而不是事后美化的记忆。

The photos will be used as opening material in Phase 3.
照片将作为阶段三的讨论起点。

---

### Phase 3: Design Probe + Reaction Session · 阶段三：设计探针反应测试
**30–45 minutes, after the diary period · 30-45分钟，日记结束后进行**

Open with the participant's own diary photos:
用参与者自己的日记照片开场：

*"Looking at this photo — what were you thinking at that moment?"*
*"看着这张照片——你当时在想什么？"*

Then present two concepts:
然后展示两个概念：

---

**Concept A: Eating Structure Intervention · 概念A：进食结构干预**
*(Environmental design · 环境设计方向)*

Food is arranged in separate sections on the plate — vegetables, protein, and carbohydrates in different areas, not mixed or stacked.
食物分开摆放在盘子上——蔬菜、蛋白质、碳水各在一个区域，不混合堆叠。

Eating order: vegetables first, then protein, then carbohydrates.
进食顺序：先蔬菜，再蛋白质，最后碳水化合物。

No phone on the table during the meal.
吃饭时桌上不放手机。

*Probe questions · 追问：*

"Is this realistic in your daily life?"
"这在你日常生活里现实吗？"

"Which part would you genuinely try? Which part would feel frustrating?"
"哪部分你愿意尝试？哪部分会让你感到烦躁？"

"Would eating this way make the experience feel better or worse?"
"这样吃饭，吃完的感受会变好还是变差？"

"What would stop you from doing this?"
"有什么东西会阻止你这样做？"

---

**Concept B: Post-Meal Body Check-In · 概念B：饭后身体觉察**
*(Non-numeric feedback · 非数字反馈方向)*

Twenty minutes after finishing a meal, a simple cue — a vibration, a small light, a brief prompt — invites you to pause and notice how your body feels right now.
吃完饭20分钟后，一个简单的提示（震动、小灯、一个问题）让你停一下，注意自己当下的身体感受。

No score. No judgement. Just: *"What does your body feel like at this moment?"*
没有打分，没有评判，只是：*"你现在的身体是什么状态？"*

*Probe questions · 追问：*

"Would this feel useful, or intrusive?"
"你觉得这会有用，还是会觉得打扰？"

"What form would you want it to take — phone notification, physical object, something else?"
"你希望它是什么形式——手机通知？实物提醒？还是别的？"

"Would it feel like surveillance, or like care?"
"它会让你感到被监控，还是被关照？"

### What each method gives you · 每个方法的数据产出

| Method · 方法 | Data · 数据 |
|--------------|------------|
| Kitchen walkthrough · 厨房导览 | Real environment: what is hidden vs visible · 真实环境：什么被藏着，什么显眼 |

## 6. Expected Contribution · 预期产出

A set of **environment and feedback design principles** answering:
一套**环境与反馈设计原则**，回答以下问题：

At which specific moments is intervention most effective and lowest friction?
在哪些具体时刻，干预最有效、摩擦最低？

Which environmental changes have low effort but real behavioural effect?
哪些环境改变门槛低但效果真实？

What forms of feedback feel supportive rather than judgmental?
什么样的反馈形式让人感到被支持，而非被评判？
