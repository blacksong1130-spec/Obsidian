# IRP Elevator Pitch

---

## ENGLISH VERSION

**The problem:**
People eat without awareness. Existing tools — calorie counters, food trackers — provide feedback that feels like surveillance, not care. They measure the wrong things and make eating more anxious, not more attuned.

**My research question:**
How can sensory design help people reconnect with their body signals around eating — without judgment, without numbers?

**What I'm designing:**
Two concepts:
- **Concept A** — Environmental design: restructuring the food space to reduce friction (what's visible, what's within reach)
- **Concept B** — Sensory feedback: a post-meal prompt at the 20-minute satiety window — light, vibration, sound, scent, or taste — that invites body awareness rather than measuring it

**What I'm testing:**
1. *Feedback acceptance threshold* — which sensory types feel like care vs. surveillance?
2. *Body signal vocabulary* — what words do people actually use to describe fullness? Richer language = richer perception
3. *Friction typology* — when people reject Concept A, is it disbelief, loss of autonomy, or too much effort? Each needs a different fix

**The ritual I'm exploring:**
Photo + voice note after each meal → system analyses nutritional colour balance → shows what to balance next meal. No scores. No calories. Just colour and language.

**Method:**
4 phases over 2–3 weeks: space observation + meal interview → 1-day photo diary → mindful lunch group → design probe session with live sensory testing.

## 中文版本

**问题：**
人们在无意识中进食。现有工具——卡路里计算器、饮食追踪 App——提供的反馈让人感觉像监控，而不是关怀。它们测量了错误的东西，让吃饭变得更焦虑，而不是更有感知。

**我的研究问题：**
感官设计如何帮助人们重新连接进食时的身体信号——不评判、不用数字？

**我在设计什么：**
两个概念：
- **Concept A** — 环境设计：重构饮食空间，降低"吃好"的摩擦（什么食物可见、什么触手可及）
- **Concept B** — 感官反馈：饭后20分钟（饱感信号到达大脑的时间窗口），用灯光、震动、音乐、气味或味道发出一个温和的提示——邀请身体感知，而不是测量它

**我在测试什么：**
1. *反馈接受阈值* — 哪种感官形式让人感到被关怀，哪种让人感到被监控？
2. *身体信号词汇表* — 人们用什么词描述饱感？语言颗粒度越细，身体感知越丰富
3. *阻力类型* — 当人们拒绝 Concept A，原因是"不信有用"、"感觉被控制"，还是"太麻烦"？三种阻力对应三种完全不同的设计修正

**我在探索的仪式：**
每顿饭后拍一张照片 + 录一段语音点评（食物味道、身体感受）→ 系统分析营养色彩比例 → 显示下一餐需要补充什么颜色。没有分数，没有卡路里，只有颜色和语言。

**方法：**
4个阶段，2-3周：空间观察 + 饭桌对话 → 一天照片日记 → 正念午餐小组 → 感官反馈设计探针访谈。
