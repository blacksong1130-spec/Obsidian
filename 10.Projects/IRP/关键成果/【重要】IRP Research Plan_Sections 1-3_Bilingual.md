# IRP Research Plan — Sections 1–3 / IRP 研究计划——第1-3部分

> Source: Revised IRP 1.pdf (2026-05-07) · Bilingual version / 来源：Revised IRP 1.pdf · 双语版本

---

## 1. Core Research Question / 核心研究问题

How can environmental and contextual design help people read their own body signals more accurately — and provide non-judgemental external reference when those signals are unclear?

环境与情境设计如何帮助人们更准确地读取自身的身体信号——并在这些信号不清晰时，提供非评判性的外部参考？

---

## 2. Target User / 目标用户

### Specific Profile / 具体画像

The target user is an adult who wants to lose weight or eat more healthily but consistently struggles — they don't know what to eat, resort to restriction (starving themselves), and feel uncertain after eating ("was that okay?"). They need reassurance that they're on the right path. This includes both home cooks and people who primarily eat takeout.

目标用户是想要减重或改善饮食习惯、但持续挣扎的成年人——他们不知道该吃什么，诉诸限制（饿自己），并在进食后感到不确定（"这样可以吗？"）。他们需要被告知自己走在正确的道路上。既包括自己做饭的人，也包括主要吃外卖的人。

### Core Insight / 核心洞察

This person needs external validation not because they lack nutritional information, but because they have lost trust in their own body's signals. The problem is **broken interoceptive trust**, not a knowledge gap. The intervention is not more data — it is rebuilding the conditions under which body signals become legible.

这类人需要外部验证，不是因为缺乏营养知识，而是因为他们已经失去了对自身身体信号的信任。问题的核心是**内感受信任的破裂**，而非知识缺口。干预的方向不是提供更多数据——而是重建让身体信号变得可读的条件。

## 3. Theoretical Framework / 理论框架

### Fogg B=MAP — Focused on A + M / Fogg 行为模型——聚焦能力与动机

The project applies Fogg's Behaviour Model (B=MAP), concentrating specifically on two components: Ability (A) and Motivation (M). The table below maps each component to a design direction and its practical meaning.

本项目采用 Fogg 行为模型（B=MAP），重点聚焦两个维度：能力（A）与动机（M）。下表将每个维度对应到一个设计方向及其实践意涵。

这个框架不是作为分析工具来用的——而是作为干预点定位器。它帮助回答：在哪里施力，以及施什么力。

| Component / 维度 | Design Direction / 设计方向 | Practical Meaning / 实践意涵 |
|---|---|---|
| **A (Ability / 能力)** | Environmental design — reduce friction for healthy choices / 环境设计——降低健康选择的摩擦力 | Change the physical context so that good choices become the default / 改变物理情境，使好的选择成为默认选项 |
| **M (Motivation / 动机)** | Non-punitive feedback — build positive felt sense / 非评判性反馈——建立积极的身体感受 | When body signals are unclear, offer a gentle external reference — not a score, but a prompt to notice / 当身体信号不清晰时，提供温和的外部参考——不是评分，而是一个"去感受"的提示 |

### Key Position: Body Signals Can Be Misleading / 核心立场：身体信号可能具有误导性

Research across multiple fields demonstrates that the body's internal signals are not straightforwardly reliable guides to eating behaviour. Four mechanisms are particularly relevant:

来自多个领域的研究表明，身体的内部信号并不是饮食行为的可靠向导。以下四个机制尤为关键：

- **Satiety delay:** Satiety signals take approximately 20 minutes to travel from the gut to the brain (Robinson et al., 2014). / **饱腹感延迟：** 饱腹感信号需要约20分钟从肠道传达到大脑（Robinson et al., 2014）。
- **Engineered bypass:** Ultra-processed foods are specifically engineered to bypass satiety mechanisms. / **人工绕过：** 超加工食品被专门设计为绕过饱腹感机制。
- **Disrupted interoception:** Chronic dieters have disrupted interoceptive signals — hunger and emotional eating become indistinguishable. / **内感受紊乱：** 长期节食者的内感受信号已被扰乱——饥饿感与情绪性进食变得难以区分。
- **False hunger cues:** Blood sugar fluctuations create false hunger signals unrelated to actual nutritional need. / **虚假饥饿信号：** 血糖波动会产生与实际营养需求无关的虚假饥饿感。

Therefore, the goal of this project is **not** "trust your body completely." It is: **design conditions where body signals become clearer, and provide gentle support when they remain ambiguous.**

因此，本项目的目标**不是**"完全信任你的身体"。而是：**设计让身体信号变得更清晰的条件，并在信号仍不明确时提供温和的支持。**
