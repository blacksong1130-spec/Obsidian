**4\. Project statement \- 200 words maximum**

==**FoodWitness starts from a simple contradiction: eating well is often framed as a matter of individual discipline, yet today’s food environments are shaped by highly processed products, persuasive marketing, and weak feedback about their health consequences. WHO identifies unhealthy diets as a major risk factor for disease, and describes obesity as a condition shaped not only by behaviour, but also by access, market forces, and the broader environment.**==

==**Through policy analysis, app audits, interviews, self-documentation, and speculative design, the project explores how personal food data might move beyond self-management. It proposes a future in which everyday acts of scanning, sensing, recording and sharing become collective evidence: helping people protect themselves now, while supporting stronger accountability across regulators, retailers, food companies, and public-health actors by 2050.**==

==**Its outputs include a future-facing evidence infrastructure, service scenarios, and public communication tools. Rather than asking individuals to be endlessly disciplined, FoodWitness asks how food systems could become more transparent, less manipulative, and easier to live within.**==
